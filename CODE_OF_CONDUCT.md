# Code of conduct

Issues on this repository are for reporting errors and changes in the law. Keep them respectful and on topic.

- Do not post confidential case records, personal information about anyone, or details of your own case.
- This reference cannot give advice about a specific case. Questions asking for legal advice will be closed; please consult a licensed attorney.
- Harassment, abuse and spam are removed.

The owner may edit, hide, lock or close any issue or comment, and may block accounts that do not follow these rules.
