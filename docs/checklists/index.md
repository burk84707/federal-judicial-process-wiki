# Federal Process Practical Checklists

These checklists prevent omitted inputs. They are not filing forms and do not establish that any remedy is available. The canonical module and verified authority record control every legal proposition.

## Universal intake checklist

- [ ] Case and district court docket numbers
- [ ] Court with present authority and controlling circuit
- [ ] Charge, conviction, and penalty statutes
- [ ] Offense, verdict or plea, sentencing, judgment, and amendment dates
- [ ] Notice, mandate, certiorari, and context-specific finality dates
- [ ] Present custody, facility, custodian, and transfer history
- [ ] Supervision commencement, conditions, transfer, violation, and expiration history
- [ ] Every prior motion, petition, appeal, authorization request, and disposition
- [ ] Exact challenged act, judgment, condition, computation, or agency decision
- [ ] Requested legal vehicle and precise relief
- [ ] All triggering events and proof of filing, receipt, service, or issuance
- [ ] Issue preservation, objections, waiver terms, default, and government invocation
- [ ] Administrative submissions, rejections, responses, extensions, and appeals
- [ ] Operative indictment, plea agreement, judgment, statement of reasons, transcripts, PSR, orders, and exhibits
- [ ] Current statute, rule, regulation, guideline, policy, published circuit authority, local rules, and negative-treatment check

## Deadline computation checklist

For every deadline, write the trigger, trigger date, excluded day, period, counted days, weekend or holiday rule, time zone and electronic-filing cutoff, extension source, tolling source, jurisdictional or claim-processing characterization, filing proof, and consequence of noncompliance. Never compute from a nearby event merely because its date is easier to find.

## Authority verification checklist

- [ ] Retrieve the current official statute, rule, regulation, guideline, policy, or opinion.
- [ ] Confirm jurisdiction, court level, posture, holding, publication status, and pinpoints.
- [ ] Separate holding from dicta and majority from concurrence or dissent.
- [ ] Check amendments, effective dates, subsequent history, abrogation, and current negative treatment.
- [ ] Check current circuit local rules, internal operating procedures, standing orders, and forms.
- [ ] Record the authority ID, source ID, official URL, retrieval date, limitations, and linked claim.
- [ ] Mark any unresolved proposition `not_verified`; do not fill the gap with inference.

## Filing-ready proof checklist

- [ ] Each requested element or exception has admissible record support.
- [ ] The filing identifies the decisionmaker with authority to grant the requested relief.
- [ ] The requested remedy matches the proven violation and does not exceed statutory discretion.
- [ ] The submission preserves alternative classifications without making contradictory factual assertions.
- [ ] The certificate, declaration, signature, service, fee or indigency materials, attachments, and local form are current.
- [ ] The appellate route, deadline, standard of review, COA status, and stay needs are recorded before filing.
