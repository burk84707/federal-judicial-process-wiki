# Eleventh Circuit Overlay

This page prevents nationwide rules from being mistaken for Eleventh Circuit doctrine and prevents unresearched circuit law from being guessed. It is a bounded overlay, not an exhaustive treatise or a substitute for the current docket, published opinions, local rules, or citator.

## Official rule source and currency

- [Official Eleventh Circuit rules and procedures](https://www.ca11.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/courtdocs/clk/Rules_Bookmarked.DEC25.pdf)
- Official December 2025 rulebook checked on 2026-08-25.
- Source authority: `AUTH-CA11-LOCAL-RULES`.
- A portal check does not prove that no issue-specific local rule exists. Open the current rule, amendments, internal operating procedures, and standing orders for the actual filing.

## Verified circuit holdings

<!-- claim:CLM-CIR11-001 authorities:AUTH-CA11-SANTIAGO-LUGO-785-F3D-467 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR11-001:** The Eleventh Circuit holds that § 2241 administrative exhaustion is not jurisdictional; it remains a requirement that a court ordinarily enforces when the respondent properly asserts it, but the court need not raise it sua sponte. [*Santiago-Lugo v. Warden*, 785 F.3d 467, 474–75 (11th Cir. 2015)](https://media.ca11.uscourts.gov/opinions/pub/files/201314384.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Eleventh Circuit. The opinion does not create a nationwide exception or excuse, and a claim-specific statutory exhaustion provision may require separate analysis.
<!-- claim:CLM-CIR11-002 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3582-3583,AUTH-CA11-CORDERO-7-F4TH-1058 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR11-002:** The Eleventh Circuit reviews denial of § 3583(e) modification or early termination for abuse of discretion and requires the order or record to show enough consideration of the relevant statutory factors to permit meaningful appellate review, without a ritual statement of every factor. [*United States v. Cordero*, 7 F.4th 1058, 1069–71 (11th Cir. 2021)](https://media.ca11.uscourts.gov/opinions/pub/files/201810837.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Eleventh Circuit; the adequacy of a particular record remains case specific and the relevant § 3553(a) subset is supplied by § 3583(e).
<!-- claim:CLM-CIR11-003 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3582-3583,AUTH-CA11-CORDERO-7-F4TH-1058 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR11-003:** The Eleventh Circuit holds that § 3583(e)(2) is not a vehicle for a collateral attack on the original legality or constitutionality of an imposed supervised-release condition; the opinion identifies direct appeal or § 2255 as the ordinary channels for such an attack. [*United States v. Cordero*, 7 F.4th 1058, 1066–68 (11th Cir. 2021)](https://media.ca11.uscourts.gov/opinions/pub/files/201810837.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Eleventh Circuit and limited to collateral attacks on the condition as originally imposed; later changed facts and permissible modification grounds require separate analysis.
<!-- claim:CLM-CIR11-004 authorities:AUTH-PL-115-391-SEC-404,AUTH-SCOTUS-CONCEPCION-597-US-481,AUTH-CA11-WILLIAMS-63-F4TH-908 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR11-004:** In the Eleventh Circuit, denial of an eligible § 404 motion is reviewed for abuse of discretion; a court may deny relief even when the existing sentence exceeds the revised statutory maximum, may not use § 404 as a collateral vehicle to invalidate the original sentence, and may satisfy Concepcion with a brief explanation evident from the record. [*United States v. Williams*, 63 F.4th 908, 912–14 (11th Cir. 2023)](https://media.ca11.uscourts.gov/opinions/pub/files/202112877.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Eleventh Circuit. Williams does not permit a court to ignore nonfrivolous arguments, require a reduction, or decide an independent collateral attack through § 404.
<!-- claim:CLM-CIR11-005 authorities:AUTH-USC-28-2253,AUTH-CA11-SAWYER-326-F3D-1363 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR11-005:** In the Eleventh Circuit, a federal prisoner proceeding under § 2241 does not need a certificate of appealability because § 2253(c)(1)(B) reaches § 2255 proceedings, not federal-process § 2241 proceedings. [*Sawyer v. Holder*, 326 F.3d 1363, 1364 n.3 (11th Cir. 2003)](https://media.ca11.uscourts.gov/opinions/pub/files/200116398.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Eleventh Circuit and limited to the COA question. Sawyer's saving-clause merits analysis is not adopted and must be read in light of later Supreme Court law; state-process custody and filing classification remain separate.
<!-- claim:CLM-CIR11-006 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3582-3583,AUTH-CA11-HARRIS-989-F3D-908 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR11-006:** In the Eleventh Circuit, § 3582(c)(1)(A)'s defendant-filing prerequisite is a nonjurisdictional claim-processing rule; when the government does not assert noncompliance, the court may reach the merits rather than raise the prerequisite on its own. [*United States v. Harris*, 989 F.3d 908, 910–12 (11th Cir. 2021)](https://media.ca11.uscourts.gov/opinions/pub/files/202012023.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Eleventh Circuit. Harris did not decide whether the filing rule is mandatory when invoked and its pandemic-specific merits analysis is not adopted as current law.
<!-- claim:CLM-CIR11-007 authorities:AUTH-USC-28-1651,AUTH-USC-28-2255,AUTH-CA11-HOLT-417-F3D-1172 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR11-007:** In the Eleventh Circuit, audita querela may not be granted when the challenge is cognizable under § 2255; after recharacterization, an unauthorized second or successive motion remains outside the district court's jurisdiction. [*United States v. Holt*, 417 F.3d 1172, 1174–75 (11th Cir. 2005) (per curiam)](https://media.ca11.uscourts.gov/opinions/pub/files/200415848.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Eleventh Circuit. Holt did not hold that audita querela survives for claims outside § 2255; it addressed a constitutional sentencing attack by a prisoner with a prior § 2255 motion and an absent successive-motion authorization.

## Application controls

1. Classify the judgment, custody source, motion, court, requested relief, and appellate posture before selecting an overlay row.
2. Use only a verified published circuit holding for a circuit proposition; do not promote a district opinion, unpublished disposition, dicta, or a party's characterization into binding law.
3. Run a current citator and check en banc, Supreme Court, statutory, rule, and local-rule changes before filing.
4. Preserve issue presentation, objections, the operative record, deadline trigger, and the governing standard of review.
5. If the issue register says `not_verified`, use the linked nationwide module only for its bounded nationwide proposition and obtain the missing controlling authority.
