# Ninth Circuit Overlay

This page prevents nationwide rules from being mistaken for Ninth Circuit doctrine and prevents unresearched circuit law from being guessed. It is a bounded overlay, not an exhaustive treatise or a substitute for the current docket, published opinions, local rules, or citator.

## Official rule source and currency

- [Official Ninth Circuit rules and procedures](https://www.ca9.uscourts.gov/rules/)
- Official rules repository showing rules effective June 1, 2026 checked on 2026-08-25.
- Source authority: `AUTH-CA9-LOCAL-RULES`.
- A portal check does not prove that no issue-specific local rule exists. Open the current rule, amendments, internal operating procedures, and standing orders for the actual filing.

## Verified circuit holdings

<!-- claim:CLM-CIR9-001 authorities:AUTH-CA9-WARD-678-F3D-1042 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR9-001:** The Ninth Circuit treats ordinary § 2241 administrative exhaustion as prudential rather than jurisdictional and recognizes waiver when administrative pursuit would be futile. [*Ward v. Chavez*, 678 F.3d 1042, 1045–46 (9th Cir. 2012)](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2012/05/08/09-17016.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Ninth Circuit and not a nationwide permission to bypass a claim-specific statutory exhaustion provision or the BOP record process.
<!-- claim:CLM-CIR9-002 authorities:AUTH-CA9-RODRIGUEZ-COPENHAVER-823-F3D-1238 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR9-002:** When BOP retained designation discretion but used an unlawful decision process, the Ninth Circuit ordered lawful reconsideration rather than the requested discretionary designation. [*Rodriguez v. Copenhaver*, 823 F.3d 1238, 1243 (9th Cir. 2016)](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2016/05/25/14-16399.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Ninth Circuit and limited to the particular § 3621(b) process error; it does not establish a universal § 2241 remedy.
<!-- claim:CLM-CIR9-003 authorities:AUTH-USC-28-1651,AUTH-CA9-CARRINGTON-503-F3D-888 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR9-003:** In the Ninth Circuit, audita querela is a gap-filling writ and is unavailable for claims cognizable under § 2255; § 2255's successive-motion restrictions or nonretroactivity do not themselves create the required gap. [*Carrington v. United States*, 503 F.3d 888, 890 (9th Cir. 2007)](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2007/09/11/0536143.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Ninth Circuit and not a nationwide elements test for audita querela.
<!-- claim:CLM-CIR9-004 authorities:AUTH-PL-115-391-SEC-404,AUTH-SCOTUS-CONCEPCION-597-US-481,AUTH-CA9-CARTER-44-F4TH-1227 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR9-004:** In the Ninth Circuit after Concepcion, a district court deciding a § 404 motion must consider the parties' nonfrivolous arguments based on intervening legal or factual developments and give a brief statement of reasons; an unexplained denial can require vacatur and remand. [*United States v. Carter*, 44 F.4th 1227, 1228–29 (9th Cir. 2022)](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2022/08/17/19-10411.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Ninth Circuit. Carter recognizes that Concepcion abrogated Kelley's contrary intervening-change restriction; it does not require a reduction or a detailed point-by-point opinion.
<!-- claim:CLM-CIR9-005 authorities:AUTH-USC-28-2253,AUTH-CA9-CLOSE-653-F3D-970 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR9-005:** In the Ninth Circuit, a person in federal custody does not need a certificate of appealability to appeal the denial of a properly classified § 2241 petition. [*Close v. Thomas*, 653 F.3d 970, 974 n.2 (9th Cir. 2011)](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2011/08/03/10-35850.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Ninth Circuit. Close involved federal BOP program-eligibility claims; the COA proposition does not decide § 2241 merits, state-process custody, § 2255, or filing classification.
<!-- claim:CLM-CIR9-006 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3582-3583,AUTH-CA9-KELLER-2-F4TH-1278 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR9-006:** In the Ninth Circuit, § 3582(c)(1)(A)'s defendant-filing prerequisite is a mandatory claim-processing rule enforced when the government invokes it; a renewed motion based on changed circumstances required a new qualifying BOP request, and the filing defect was not cured by the passage of 30 days while the motion was pending. [*United States v. Keller*, 2 F.4th 1278, 1281–84 (9th Cir. 2021)](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2021/07/01/20-50247.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Ninth Circuit and limited to a later motion premised on changed circumstances. Keller does not establish a universal issue-exhaustion test for every supplemental fact or argument.
<!-- claim:CLM-CIR9-007 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3582-3583,AUTH-CA9-PONCE-22-F4TH-1045 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR9-007:** In the Ninth Circuit, § 3583(e)(1) does not impose a threshold requirement of exceptional or extraordinary circumstances, exceptionally good behavior, or undue hardship before a court may consider early termination. [*United States v. Ponce*, 22 F.4th 1045, 1047–49 (9th Cir. 2022)](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2022/01/11/21-30009.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Ninth Circuit. Ponce remanded because the applied standard was unclear, did not hold compliance sufficient, and expressly did not reach the separate explanation challenge.

## Application controls

1. Classify the judgment, custody source, motion, court, requested relief, and appellate posture before selecting an overlay row.
2. Use only a verified published circuit holding for a circuit proposition; do not promote a district opinion, unpublished disposition, dicta, or a party's characterization into binding law.
3. Run a current citator and check en banc, Supreme Court, statutory, rule, and local-rule changes before filing.
4. Preserve issue presentation, objections, the operative record, deadline trigger, and the governing standard of review.
5. If the issue register says `not_verified`, use the linked nationwide module only for its bounded nationwide proposition and obtain the missing controlling authority.
