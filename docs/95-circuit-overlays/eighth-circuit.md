# Eighth Circuit Overlay

This page prevents nationwide rules from being mistaken for Eighth Circuit doctrine and prevents unresearched circuit law from being guessed. It is a bounded overlay, not an exhaustive treatise or a substitute for the current docket, published opinions, local rules, or citator.

## Official rule source and currency

- [Official Eighth Circuit rules and procedures](https://www.ca8.uscourts.gov/rules-procedures)
- Official rules-and-procedures repository checked on 2026-08-25; the linked local-rules PDF is dated June 17, 2024.
- Source authority: `AUTH-CA8-LOCAL-RULES`.
- A portal check does not prove that no issue-specific local rule exists. Open the current rule, amendments, internal operating procedures, and standing orders for the actual filing.

## Verified circuit holdings

<!-- claim:CLM-CIR8-001 authorities:AUTH-CA8-MATHENA-577-F3D-943 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR8-001:** In the Eighth Circuit, a prisoner challenging the BOP's execution of a federal sentence must first present the claim to the BOP; failure to request the available BOP designation required dismissal of the § 2241 petition without prejudice. [*Mathena v. United States*, 577 F.3d 943, 946 (8th Cir. 2009)](https://ecf.ca8.uscourts.gov/opndir/09/08/082184P.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Eighth Circuit and limited to the available nunc pro tunc designation process in the sentence-execution posture. No general exception rule is inferred.
<!-- claim:CLM-CIR8-002 authorities:AUTH-PL-115-391-SEC-404,AUTH-SCOTUS-CONCEPCION-597-US-481,AUTH-CA8-MCDONALD-944-F3D-769 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR8-002:** In the Eighth Circuit, eligibility under § 404 is reviewed de novo and the grant or denial of an eligible motion is reviewed for abuse of discretion; the First Step Act does not require a hearing on the motion. [*United States v. McDonald*, 944 F.3d 769, 771–72 (8th Cir. 2019)](https://ecf.ca8.uscourts.gov/opndir/19/12/191221P.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Eighth Circuit. McDonald's no-hearing and review holdings survive as bounded propositions; any pre-Concepcion restriction on intervening information is not adopted.
<!-- claim:CLM-CIR8-003 authorities:AUTH-USC-28-2253,AUTH-CA8-LANGELLA-612-F3D-938 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR8-003:** In the Eighth Circuit, the certificate-of-appealability requirement does not apply to a federal prisoner effectively filing under § 2241. [*Langella v. Anderson*, 612 F.3d 938, 939 n.2 (8th Cir. 2010)](https://ecf.ca8.uscourts.gov/opndir/10/07/093024P.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Eighth Circuit. Langella involved federal parole detention; no rule is adopted for state-process custody, § 2255, or disputed classification of a mislabeled filing.
<!-- claim:CLM-CIR8-004 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3582-3583,AUTH-CA8-HOUCK-2-F4TH-1082 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR8-004:** In the Eighth Circuit, § 3582(c)(1)(A)'s filing prerequisite is a mandatory claim-processing rule that must be enforced when properly raised; an unexhausted motion is dismissed without prejudice, and asserted futility based on the Attorney General's home-confinement memorandum did not excuse the missing request. [*United States v. Houck*, 2 F.4th 1082, 1084–85 (8th Cir. 2021)](https://ecf.ca8.uscourts.gov/opndir/21/06/202216P.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Eighth Circuit. Houck's futility holding is limited to the theory actually presented; compassionate release and BOP home-confinement placement remain distinct remedies.
<!-- claim:CLM-CIR8-005 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3582-3583,AUTH-FRCRIM-P-32-1,AUTH-CA8-NORRIS-62-F4TH-441 status:verified_current -->
- **CLM-CIR8-005:** In the Eighth Circuit, Rule 32.1(c)(1) does not require counsel or a hearing before a court denies a motion to terminate supervision or modify conditions, and § 3583(e) does not require a separate explanation for denial when the existing record makes the court's basis discernible. [*United States v. Norris*, 62 F.4th 441, 445–51 (8th Cir. 2023)](https://ecf.ca8.uscourts.gov/opndir/23/03/213849P.pdf)

  Limitation: Binding only in the Eighth Circuit and limited to denial. Rule 32.1 ordinarily requires a hearing and counsel before an actual condition modification unless an enumerated exception applies; Norris does not authorize an unexplained denial when the record supplies no discernible basis.

## Application controls

1. Classify the judgment, custody source, motion, court, requested relief, and appellate posture before selecting an overlay row.
2. Use only a verified published circuit holding for a circuit proposition; do not promote a district opinion, unpublished disposition, dicta, or a party's characterization into binding law.
3. Run a current citator and check en banc, Supreme Court, statutory, rule, and local-rule changes before filing.
4. Preserve issue presentation, objections, the operative record, deadline trigger, and the governing standard of review.
5. If the issue register says `not_verified`, use the linked nationwide module only for its bounded nationwide proposition and obtain the missing controlling authority.
