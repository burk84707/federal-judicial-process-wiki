# Controlled Federal Process Glossary

These are routing definitions. Each definition is bounded by its source map and cannot replace the linked canonical claim, current authority, jurisdiction, or procedural posture.

| Term | Controlled definition | Canonical source | Claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Abuse of discretion** | An appellate-review label whose content depends on the issue and governing authority; it is not a substitute for identifying legal, factual, and procedural components. | [Review Briefing And Anders](../50-direct-review/review-briefing-and-anders.md) | `CLM-REVIEW-014` |
| **Administrative exhaustion** | Completion or required use of an agency review path before judicial relief; its source, character, waiver, excuse, and remedy must be identified for the particular claim. | [Administrative Remedies And Judicial Review](../80-sentence-execution-and-bop/administrative-remedies-and-judicial-review.md) | `CLM-BOPAR-016` |
| **Anders procedure** | The appellate procedure used when appointed counsel concludes an appeal is frivolous, subject to the governing Supreme Court and circuit process. | [Review Briefing And Anders](../50-direct-review/review-briefing-and-anders.md) | `CLM-REVIEW-030` |
| **Appeal waiver** | An agreement term whose text, knowing-and-voluntary record, scope, exceptions, government invocation, and controlling circuit law must be analyzed. | [Notice Jurisdiction And Record](../50-direct-review/notice-jurisdiction-and-record.md) | `CLM-APPEAL-018` |
| **Audita querela** | A residual common-law writ claimed as gap filling; availability and any genuine gap require current controlling circuit authority. | [Extraordinary Writs Coram Nobis Audita Querela And Mandamus](../60-post-conviction/extraordinary-writs-coram-nobis-audita-querela-and-mandamus.md) | `CLM-XWRIT-020` |
| **Binding authority** | Authority controlling the court and issue in the actual jurisdiction and posture, subject to later statutory, rule, en banc, or Supreme Court change. | [Supreme Court](../95-circuit-overlays/supreme-court.md) | module-level control |
| **BOP administrative remedy** | The federal Bureau of Prisons review process in 28 C.F.R. part 542; it is an agency path and does not itself decide every judicial exhaustion question. | [Administrative Remedies And Judicial Review](../80-sentence-execution-and-bop/administrative-remedies-and-judicial-review.md) | `CLM-BOPAR-001` |
| **Certificate of appealability** | The statutory appellate gate in 28 U.S.C. § 2253(c), classified by the proceeding and source of detention rather than by the word habeas alone. | [2241 Appeal And Relief](../60-post-conviction/2241-appeal-and-relief.md) | `CLM-2241-001` |
| **Claim-processing rule** | A mandatory procedural rule when properly invoked that is not necessarily a limit on subject-matter jurisdiction. | [Rule 33 New Trial](../30-plea-and-trial/rule-33-new-trial.md) | `CLM-R33-009` |
| **Compassionate release** | The § 3582(c)(1)(A) modification vehicle, with a Bureau or defendant motion route and statutory defendant-motion gate. | [Section 3582 Remedy Router](../70-sentence-modification/section-3582-remedy-router.md) | `CLM-3582-008` |
| **Controlling circuit** | The federal court of appeals whose published current law governs the district court or appeal in the actual case. | [Index](../95-circuit-overlays/index.md) | module-level control |
| **Coram nobis** | An extraordinary criminal postjudgment writ for a bounded no-custody posture under Supreme Court and controlling circuit law; it is not an automatic substitute for § 2255. | [Extraordinary Writs Coram Nobis Audita Querela And Mandamus](../60-post-conviction/extraordinary-writs-coram-nobis-audita-querela-and-mandamus.md) | `CLM-XWRIT-016` |
| **Direct appeal** | Review from an appealable criminal judgment or order through the current appellate rules and jurisdiction statutes. | [Index](../50-direct-review/index.md) | module-level control |
| **Finality** | A context-specific legal status; the event relevant to appeal, § 2255 limitations, retroactivity, or another rule must be identified rather than treated as one universal date. | [Rehearing Mandate Certiorari And Finality](../50-direct-review/rehearing-mandate-certiorari-and-finality.md) | `CLM-FINAL-019` |
| **First Step Act time credits** | Credits earned under § 3632(d)(4) and applied, if separately eligible, under § 3624(g); earning and application are distinct. | [First Step Act Time Credits](../80-sentence-execution-and-bop/first-step-act-time-credits.md) | `CLM-FTC-004` |
| **Good-conduct time** | Credit under 18 U.S.C. § 3624(b), distinct from days physically served and from First Step Act time credits. | [Good Conduct Time](../80-sentence-execution-and-bop/good-conduct-time.md) | `CLM-GCT-002` |
| **Harmless error** | A review framework whose burden and formulation depend on the preserved error and governing source. | [Review Briefing And Anders](../50-direct-review/review-briefing-and-anders.md) | `CLM-REVIEW-009` |
| **Immediate custodian** | The default proper respondent for a core challenge to present physical custody, ordinarily the facility warden. | [2241 Filing Jurisdiction Respondent And Exhaustion](../60-post-conviction/2241-filing-jurisdiction-respondent-and-exhaustion.md) | `CLM-2241F-011` |
| **Jurisdiction** | The court's legal power to adjudicate the proceeding; it must not be conflated with venue, personal-jurisdiction-like habeas limits, merits, or a claim-processing rule. | [Index](../00-system-overview/index.md) | module-level control |
| **Local rule** | A court's current local procedural rule, used with the national statute or rule and checked for current amendments and standing orders. | [Index](../95-circuit-overlays/index.md) | module-level control |
| **Mandate** | The court of appeals' formal act returning or concluding authority under Appellate Rule 41; it is distinct from judgment entry and certiorari timing. | [Rehearing Mandate Certiorari And Finality](../50-direct-review/rehearing-mandate-certiorari-and-finality.md) | `CLM-FINAL-010` |
| **Mootness** | The Article III requirement for a live, redressable dispute throughout litigation, analyzed against the actual injury and requested relief. | [2241 Saving Clause Custody And Mootness](../60-post-conviction/2241-saving-clause-custody-and-mootness.md) | `CLM-2241S-030` |
| **New judgment** | A judgment classification relevant to later-application analysis; the actual judgment and scope of resentencing must be examined under controlling authority. | [2255 Later Filings Classification And Authorization](../60-post-conviction/2255-later-filings-classification-and-authorization.md) | `CLM-SOS-003` |
| **Plain error** | The appellate framework for forfeited error under Rule 52(b), with separate required showings rather than automatic relief. | [Review Briefing And Anders](../50-direct-review/review-briefing-and-anders.md) | `CLM-REVIEW-011` |
| **Precedential publication status** | Whether an opinion is published and binding in the relevant court system; publication does not cure jurisdictional mismatch or later negative treatment. | [Index](../95-circuit-overlays/index.md) | module-level control |
| **Procedural default** | A bar or limitation based on failure to raise a claim at the required time, subject to the governing vehicle's cause, prejudice, innocence, waiver, and jurisdiction rules. | [2255 Default Innocence And Ineffective Assistance](../60-post-conviction/2255-default-innocence-and-ineffective-assistance.md) | `CLM-2255D-001` |
| **Remand** | An appellate disposition returning a matter to a lower court with instructions or a defined scope; it is not the same as final merits relief. | [Rehearing Mandate Certiorari And Finality](../50-direct-review/rehearing-mandate-certiorari-and-finality.md) | `CLM-FINAL-012` |
| **Remedy** | The legally authorized relief tied to the proven violation and decisionmaker; eligibility for a vehicle is not entitlement to every requested outcome. | [Index](../decision-trees/index.md) | module-level control |
| **Rule 33 motion** | A defendant's federal criminal new-trial motion, with separate filing periods for newly discovered evidence and other grounds. | [Rule 33 New Trial](../30-plea-and-trial/rule-33-new-trial.md) | `CLM-R33-003` |
| **Rule 35** | The criminal rule containing a narrow court-correction route and government-motion sentence-reduction routes; subsection, trigger, and timing control. | [Rules 35 36 And Modification Appeals](../70-sentence-modification/rules-35-36-and-modification-appeals.md) | `CLM-R3536-001` |
| **Rule 36** | The rule permitting correction of a clerical error in a judgment, order, or record, not a general substantive resentencing vehicle. | [Rules 35 36 And Modification Appeals](../70-sentence-modification/rules-35-36-and-modification-appeals.md) | `CLM-R3536-028` |
| **Saving clause** | Section 2255(e)'s limited clause addressed by Jones v. Hendrix; it is not a general route around § 2255 restrictions. | [2241 Saving Clause Custody And Mootness](../60-post-conviction/2241-saving-clause-custody-and-mootness.md) | `CLM-2241S-001` |
| **Second or successive** | A statutory classification that is not equivalent to second in time and depends on judgment, claim, ripeness, and prior disposition. | [2255 Later Filings Classification And Authorization](../60-post-conviction/2255-later-filings-classification-and-authorization.md) | `CLM-SOS-001` |
| **Standard of review** | The appellate lens for the specific issue, preservation status, and posture; a case can contain different standards for different questions. | [Review Briefing And Anders](../50-direct-review/review-briefing-and-anders.md) | `CLM-REVIEW-001` |
| **Supervised release** | A post-imprisonment component of the federal sentence governed principally by § 3583, distinct from parole and from imprisonment. | [Commencement Conditions Modification Termination And Transfer](../90-supervised-release-and-reentry/commencement-conditions-modification-termination-and-transfer.md) | `CLM-SRTERM-001` |
| **Venue** | The legally proper geographic place for a prosecution or proceeding; it is distinct from subject-matter jurisdiction and from § 2241's custodian rule. | [Federal Charging And Commencement](../10-investigation-and-charging/federal-charging-and-commencement.md) | `CLM-CHARGE-028` |
| **Waiver** | Intentional relinquishment or an agreement-based limit whose source, scope, record, exceptions, and government invocation must be identified. | [Notice Jurisdiction And Record](../50-direct-review/notice-jurisdiction-and-record.md) | `CLM-APPEAL-018` |
| **§ 2241** | The federal habeas statute used in several custody postures; respondent, district, exhaustion, merits, and remedy depend on the custody and claim classification. | [2241 Filing Jurisdiction Respondent And Exhaustion](../60-post-conviction/2241-filing-jurisdiction-respondent-and-exhaustion.md) | `CLM-2241F-001` |
| **§ 2255** | The federal prisoner's sentencing-court motion vehicle for the grounds and remedies stated in the statute, subject to custody, timing, procedural, and later-application rules. | [2255 Initial Motion And Limitations](../60-post-conviction/2255-initial-motion-and-limitations.md) | `CLM-2255-001` |
| **§ 3582(c)(2)** | A sentence-modification vehicle for a qualifying retroactive Guidelines reduction consistent with Commission policy, not a plenary resentencing. | [Section 3582 Remedy Router](../70-sentence-modification/section-3582-remedy-router.md) | `CLM-3582-018` |

## Use rule

When a term has different meanings across statutes, rules, circuits, or postures, use the exact governing source and full phrase rather than this shorthand. Terms marked by a module-level control require proposition-level research before being used as law.
