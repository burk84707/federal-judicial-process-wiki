# System Overview
How the canonical, generated, migration, and validation layers fit together.
This page is a verified navigation layer. The linked canonical modules, atomic claims, authority cards, and structured controls remain the legal source; this index does not create a rule from omission or shorthand.
## Canonical-source hierarchy
1. `docs/` modular source pages and their claim markers.
2. `data/claims.jsonl`, `data/authorities.jsonl`, and the structured deadline, jurisdiction, standard, remedy, and graph controls.
3. `authorities/` cards and `sources/source_registry.yaml`.
4. Generated master, site, diagram, RAG, Confluence, Jira, HTML, and PDF artifacts.
5. Legacy materials, which are preserved evidence of what was checked and never authority for current law.
## Required intake before routing
Identify jurisdiction, court, procedural posture, judgment and finality dates, present custody and location, challenged decision, requested remedy, pending proceedings, prior applications and dispositions, deadlines and triggers, issue preservation, controlling circuit, local rules, and the complete source record.
## Verification stops
A `not_verified`, `disputed`, or `requires_citator_check` record cannot be converted into a legal answer. A verified nationwide claim cannot be extended into circuit doctrine or an individual outcome without the missing record and controlling authority.
