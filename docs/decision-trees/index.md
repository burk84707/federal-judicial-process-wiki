# Federal Process Decision Trees

These trees classify a federal procedural problem; they do not predict an outcome. Each route ends at a canonical module, and the linked module and claim records control over the shorthand.

## Whole-case router

| Routing question | Route | Canonical module | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| The criminal case has not reached final judgment | Use the charging, pretrial, plea/trial, sentencing, or direct-review stage that matches the actual docket posture. | [Index](../10-investigation-and-charging/index.md) | Do not use post-conviction labels to bypass a pending criminal proceeding. |
| A verdict or finding is challenged before or around judgment | Classify Rule 29, mistrial, verdict, juror, Rule 33, sentencing, and notice-of-appeal timing separately. | [Rule 29, Mistrial, Verdict, and Juror Misconduct](../30-plea-and-trial/rule-29-mistrial-verdict-and-juror-misconduct.md) | Record verdict, filing, sentencing, judgment, and appeal dates. |
| The federal conviction or sentence itself is attacked while the movant is in custody | Start with the initial § 2255 modules in the sentencing court. | [Initial Section 2255 Motion, Filing, and Limitations](../60-post-conviction/2255-initial-motion-and-limitations.md) | Check custody, limitations trigger, waiver/default, record, and requested remedy. |
| A later filing again attacks a federal judgment | Classify the judgment, claim, ripeness, and prior disposition before calling it second or successive. | [Later Section 2255 Filings — Classification and Authorization](../60-post-conviction/2255-later-filings-classification-and-authorization.md) | Do not classify by paper count alone. |
| The challenge concerns present execution of a federal sentence | Classify the challenged BOP action, respondent, district, remedy record, and § 2241 route. | [28 U.S.C. § 2241 — Filing, Custodian, District, and Administrative Exhaustion](../60-post-conviction/2241-filing-jurisdiction-respondent-and-exhaustion.md) | Separate execution from judgment validity and conditions claims. |
| The requested relief is a statutory sentence modification | Route among § 3582(c)(1)(A), § 3582(c)(2), § 3582(c)(1)(B), First Step Act § 404, and Rules 35/36. | [18 U.S.C. § 3582 — Sentence-Modification Remedy Router](../70-sentence-modification/section-3582-remedy-router.md) | Vehicle, gate, eligible change, remedy, and appeal rules are not interchangeable. |
| The issue is BOP computation, credits, designation, discipline, programming, or placement | Use the sentence-execution modules and preserve the complete agency record. | [Index](../80-sentence-execution-and-bop/index.md) | Distinguish eligibility, earning, application, discretion, and reviewability. |
| The issue arises during supervised release | Separate commencement, conditions, modification, early termination, transfer, violation, revocation, and collateral challenge. | [Index](../90-supervised-release-and-reentry/index.md) | Use the current condition text, supervision chronology, docket, and circuit law. |
| Custody has ended but a conviction consequence remains | Classify coram nobis, executive clemency, and consequence-specific nonjudicial remedies separately. | [Extraordinary Writs — Coram Nobis, Audita Querela, and Mandamus](../60-post-conviction/extraordinary-writs-coram-nobis-audita-querela-and-mandamus.md) | No-custody status alone does not establish a writ or a consequence remedy. |
| A circuit-specific procedural or merits question remains | Use the Supreme Court floor and the circuit with jurisdiction; stop at every not-verified row. | [Index](../95-circuit-overlays/index.md) | Run a current citator and current local-rule check before filing. |

## Stage sequence

```text
Federal process root
  -> investigation and charging
  -> pretrial
  -> plea or trial
  -> sentencing and judgment
  -> direct review and finality
  -> post-conviction, modification, or sentence execution
  -> supervised release and reentry
Every branch -> jurisdiction + deadline + record + remedy + circuit overlay checks
```

## Mandatory final check

Before selecting any route, check pending proceedings, preservation, timeliness, exhaustion, custody, mootness, proper respondent, venue, successive consequences, waiver, standard of review, available remedy, appealability, COA, local rules, controlling circuit law, and the evidence needed to prove each factual predicate.
