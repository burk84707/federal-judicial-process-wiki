# 18 U.S.C. § 3632 — First Step Act Time Credits

## Verification boundary

This module verifies the current nationwide earning and application structure for First Step Act time credits (FTCs). It does not calculate a particular prisoner's credits, resolve an aggregated-sentence dispute, validate a PATTERN score, decide an offense under § 3632(d)(4)(D), or establish exhaustion, habeas jurisdiction, venue, or relief under 28 U.S.C. § 2241.

The legacy-control instruction calls for verification of a legacy “Maxwell” reference. Exhaustive repository and legacy-corpus searches found that name only in the instruction itself, not in any legal-content file. No Maxwell case identity, citation, proposition, or holding is therefore asserted here.

## System development and reassessment

<!-- claim:CLM-FTC-001 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3632 status:verified_current -->
Section 3632(a) required the Attorney General, in consultation with the Independent Review Committee, to develop and publicly release the risk-and-needs assessment system no later than 210 days after enactment. [18 U.S.C. § 3632(a)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/3632)

<!-- claim:CLM-FTC-002 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3632 status:verified_current -->
The system must reassess each prisoner's recidivism risk periodically using indicators of progress and regression that are dynamic and reasonably expected to change in prison. [18 U.S.C. § 3632(a)(4)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/3632)

<!-- claim:CLM-FTC-003 authorities:AUTH-DOJ-PATTERN-2019-RELEASE status:verified_historical -->
The Department of Justice published the First Step Act Risk and Needs Assessment System on July 19, 2019; the new tool the Bureau of Prisons would use under that system is called PATTERN, and DOJ described the publication as a key requirement of the First Step Act. [U.S. Department of Justice PATTERN publication record (July 19, 2019)](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/department-justice-announces-release-3100-inmates-under-first-step-act-publishes-risk-and) [DOJ release (July 19, 2019)](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/department-justice-announces-release-3100-inmates-under-first-step-act-publishes-risk-and); [NIJ Director's implementation remarks](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/speech/national-institute-justice-director-david-muhlhausen-delivers-remarks-first-step-act)

The later January 2022 time-credit regulation is not evidence that the separate 210-day system-development and publication deadline was missed.

## Earning credits

<!-- claim:CLM-FTC-004 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3632,AUTH-CFR-28-523-SUBPART-E status:verified_current -->
Except for a prisoner made ineligible by § 3632(d)(4)(D), successful participation earns ten days of FTCs for every thirty days of qualifying evidence-based recidivism-reduction programming or productive activities. [18 U.S.C. § 3632(d)(4)(A)(i)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/3632); [28 C.F.R. § 523.42(c)(1)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/chapter-V/subchapter-B/part-523/subpart-E)

<!-- claim:CLM-FTC-005 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3632,AUTH-CFR-28-523-SUBPART-E status:verified_current -->
A prisoner earns an additional five days of time credits for every thirty days of successful participation if the Bureau determines the prisoner to be at minimum or low risk for recidivating and, in the statute's words, the prisoner "over 2 consecutive assessments, has not increased their risk of recidivism"; the regulation words this condition differently, requiring that the inmate have "maintained a consistent minimum or low risk of recidivism over the most recent two consecutive risk and needs assessments." [18 U.S.C. § 3632(d)(4)(A)(ii)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/3632); [28 C.F.R. § 523.42(c)(2)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title28-vol2/pdf/CFR-2025-title28-vol2-part523.pdf) [18 U.S.C. § 3632(d)(4)(A)(ii)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/3632); [28 C.F.R. § 523.42(c)(2)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/chapter-V/subchapter-B/part-523/subpart-E)

<!-- claim:CLM-FTC-006 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3632 status:verified_current -->
The statute bars credit for a program successfully completed before December 21, 2018 and for a program completed during official detention before the prisoner's sentence commences under § 3585(a). [18 U.S.C. § 3632(d)(4)(B)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/3632)

<!-- claim:CLM-FTC-007 authorities:AUTH-CFR-28-523-SUBPART-E status:verified_current -->
The regulation allows an otherwise eligible inmate to earn for programming and activities from December 21, 2018 through January 14, 2020; beginning January 15, 2020, earning requires successful participation in programs or activities recommended from the individualized risk-and-needs assessment. [28 C.F.R. § 523.42(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/chapter-V/subchapter-B/part-523/subpart-E)

<!-- claim:CLM-FTC-008 authorities:AUTH-CFR-28-523-SUBPART-E-CURRENT,AUTH-FR-91-55740 status:verified_current -->
Under the regulation as amended effective September 30, 2026, an eligible inmate begins earning FSA Time Credits after the inmate's term of imprisonment commences; the amendment removed the former parenthetical that identified that date as the date the inmate arrives or voluntarily surrenders at the designated Bureau facility where the sentence will be served. [28 C.F.R. § 523.42(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/chapter-V/subchapter-B/part-523/subpart-E); [91 Fed. Reg. 55,740, 55,741, 55,743 (Aug. 31, 2026) (interim final rule)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-08-31/pdf/2026-17752.pdf)

## Eligibility to earn

<!-- claim:CLM-FTC-009 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3632,AUTH-CFR-28-523-SUBPART-E status:verified_current -->
Section 3632(d)(4)(D) makes a prisoner ineligible to earn FTCs when the prisoner is serving a sentence for a conviction under an enumerated provision; the current exact statutory list—not a category label or a fixed estimate of the number of offenses—controls the federal offense screen. [18 U.S.C. § 3632(d)(4)(D)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/3632); [28 C.F.R. § 523.41(d)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/chapter-V/subchapter-B/part-523/subpart-E)

An individualized screen requires the judgment, conviction statutes, sentence structure, and current governing authority. This module does not resolve how an aggregated term containing eligible and ineligible counts is treated.

<!-- claim:CLM-FTC-010 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3632,AUTH-CFR-28-523-SUBPART-E status:verified_current -->
A final removal order is an application bar, not one of § 3632(d)(4)(D)'s earning exclusions: the regulation's earning rule excludes the enumerated-offense category, while § 3632(d)(4)(E) and § 523.44(a)(2) prohibit applying FTCs for a prisoner subject to a final order of removal. [18 U.S.C. § 3632(d)(4)(D)–(E)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/3632); [28 C.F.R. §§ 523.41(d), 523.44(a)(2)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/chapter-V/subchapter-B/part-523/subpart-E)

## Application is a separate gate

<!-- claim:CLM-FTC-011 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3632,AUTH-USC-18-3624 status:verified_current -->
Section 3632(d)(4)(C) directs that earned FTCs be applied toward prerelease custody or supervised release and requires the Director to transfer eligible prisoners, as determined under § 3624(g); earning a credit and qualifying to apply it are therefore separate determinations. For supervised release, § 3624(g)(3) provides that the Director "may transfer" the prisoner to begin it early, "not to exceed 12 months," and a prisoner subject to a final order of removal is ineligible to apply time credits. [18 U.S.C. § 3632(d)(4)(C), (E)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/3632); [18 U.S.C. § 3624(g)(1), (g)(3)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/3624) [18 U.S.C. § 3632(d)(4)(C)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/3632); [18 U.S.C. § 3624(g)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/3624)

<!-- claim:CLM-FTC-012 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3624,AUTH-CFR-28-523-SUBPART-E status:verified_current -->
Before application, the prisoner must have credits equal to the remainder of the imposed term, must have demonstrated risk reduction or maintained minimum or low risk during imprisonment, and must have had the remainder of the term computed under applicable law. [18 U.S.C. § 3624(g)(1)(A)–(C)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/3624); [28 C.F.R. § 523.44(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/chapter-V/subchapter-B/part-523/subpart-E)

<!-- claim:CLM-FTC-013 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3624,AUTH-CFR-28-523-SUBPART-E status:verified_current -->
For prerelease custody, § 3624(g) and § 523.44(c) additionally require minimum or low risk in the last two assessments, unless the warden approves the statutory petition after making the required danger, good-faith-effort, and recidivism findings. [18 U.S.C. § 3624(g)(1)(D)(i)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/3624); [28 C.F.R. § 523.44(c)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/chapter-V/subchapter-B/part-523/subpart-E)

<!-- claim:CLM-FTC-014 authorities:AUTH-USC-18-3624,AUTH-CFR-28-523-SUBPART-E status:verified_current -->
Under the regulation, the Bureau may apply FTCs toward early transfer to supervised release only when the inmate meets the general application criteria in § 523.44(b) and the prerelease-custody criteria in § 523.44(c) (minimum or low risk through the last two assessments, or an approved warden petition), has maintained minimum or low risk through the last assessment, and has a court-imposed supervised-release term; the transfer may be no earlier than 12 months before it would otherwise have occurred. The statute likewise provides that the Director "may transfer" the prisoner to supervised release early, "not to exceed 12 months." [18 U.S.C. § 3624(g)(1)(D)(ii), (g)(3)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/3624); [28 C.F.R. § 523.44(b)–(d)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title28-vol2/pdf/CFR-2025-title28-vol2-part523.pdf) [18 U.S.C. § 3624(g)(1)(D)(ii)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/3624); [28 C.F.R. § 523.44(d)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/chapter-V/subchapter-B/part-523/subpart-E)

## Loss, review, and agency implementation

<!-- claim:CLM-FTC-015 authorities:AUTH-CFR-28-523-SUBPART-E status:verified_current -->
An inmate may lose earned FTCs for violating program or activity requirements under Part 541 procedures, may seek review through the Bureau's Part 542 Administrative Remedy Program, and may receive case-specific restoration after clear conduct for two consecutive risk-and-needs assessments. [28 C.F.R. § 523.43](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/chapter-V/subchapter-B/part-523/subpart-E)

<!-- claim:CLM-FTC-016 authorities:AUTH-BOP-PS-5410-01-CN2 status:verified_current -->
BOP Program Statement 5410.01, Change Notice 2, dated March 10, 2023, is official agency implementation policy for § 3632(d)(4); it does not displace the statute or regulation. [BOP Program Statement 5410.01 CN-2](https://www.bop.gov/policy/progstat/5410.01_cn2.pdf)

## Individual audit checklist

1. Obtain the judgment, conviction statutes, presentence report where lawfully available, and Bureau FTC worksheet.
2. Separate eligibility to earn, actual successful-participation days, the ten- versus fifteen-day rate, loss or restoration, and eligibility to apply.
3. Check the precise current text of § 3632(d)(4)(D); do not rely on an offense label or an estimated number of categories.
4. Identify any final removal order, detainer, non-U.S.-Code sentence issue, PATTERN assessments, and warden-petition record.
5. Compare the worksheet against §§ 523.41–523.44 and the current Program Statement.
6. Preserve all administrative-remedy submissions, receipts, rejections, extensions, and responses before researching a § 2241 claim.
