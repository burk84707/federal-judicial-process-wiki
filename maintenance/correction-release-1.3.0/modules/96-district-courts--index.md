#### CLM-DISTRICT-MAP-001: rewritten

Before (public 1.2.1):

> The federal judicial system has 94 district-level courts: 91 Article III district courts and three Article IV territorial courts—the District Court of Guam, District Court for the Northern Mariana Islands, and District Court of the Virgin Islands. [Administrative Office of the U.S. Courts]

After:

> The federal judicial system has 94 federal judicial districts: all but three have lifetime-appointed Article III judges, and judges serve 10-year terms in three territorial districts—the U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, and the Northern Mariana Islands. [Administrative Office of the U.S. Courts]

#### CLM-DISTRICT-MAP-002: rewritten

Before (public 1.2.1):

> Federal courts may prescribe local rules under 28 U.S.C. § 2071 and the applicable national procedural rule, and federal courts must post local rules on their public websites. [Administrative Office of the U.S. Courts]

After:

> United States district courts and courts of appeals may prescribe local rules under 28 U.S.C. § 2071(a)–(b) and the applicable national procedural rule; such rules must be consistent with Acts of Congress and the Federal Rules of Practice and Procedure and may be prescribed only after notice and an opportunity for public comment.
