#### CLM-GCT-013: rewritten

Before (public 1.2.1):

> Good-conduct-time credit under § 3624(b) is distinct from First Step Act time credits earned through recidivism-reduction programs and applied under §§ 3632(d)(4) and 3624(g).

After:

> Good-conduct-time credit under § 3624(b) is distinct from First Step Act time credits, which are earned through evidence-based recidivism reduction programming or productive activities under § 3632(d)(4) and applied toward prerelease custody or supervised release under § 3624(g). [18 U.S.C. § 3624(b), (g)]; [18 U.S.C. § 3632(d)(4), (d)(6)]; [28 C.F.R. § 523.20]
