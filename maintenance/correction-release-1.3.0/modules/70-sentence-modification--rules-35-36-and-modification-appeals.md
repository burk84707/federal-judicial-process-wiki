#### CLM-R3536-003: rewritten

Before (public 1.2.1):

> Classify the requested act before filing: correction of a qualifying clear sentencing error under Rule 35(a), reduction for qualifying assistance on the government’s motion under Rule 35(b), or correction of a clerical record error under Rule 36. [Fed. R. Crim. P. 35–36]

#### CLM-R3536-006: rewritten
