#### CLM-SENT-033: rewritten

Before (public 1.2.1):

> In deciding whether, how much, and how to impose a fine, the court must consider § 3553(a) and § 3572(a)'s additional factors; if restitution is ordered, payment to victims takes priority over payment of the fine. [18 U.S.C. § 3572(a), (b)]

After:

> In determining whether to impose a fine, and its amount, time for payment, and method of payment, the court shall consider § 3572(a)'s factors in addition to the § 3553(a) factors; if the defendant must make restitution to a victim of the offense other than the United States, the court shall impose a fine or other monetary penalty only to the extent it will not impair the defendant's ability to make restitution. [18 U.S.C. § 3572(a), (b)]

#### CLM-SENT-034: rewritten

Before (public 1.2.1):

> The *Apprendi* rule applies to criminal fines, so a fact necessary to raise the statutory maximum fine must be admitted or found by a jury beyond a reasonable doubt. [*Southern Union Co. v. United States*, 567 U.S. 343, 346, 350–60 (2012)]

After:

> The *Apprendi* rule applies to criminal fines, so a fact necessary to raise the statutory maximum fine must be admitted or found by a jury beyond a reasonable doubt; where a fine is so insubstantial that the underlying offense is considered petty, the Sixth Amendment jury-trial right is not triggered and no *Apprendi* issue arises. [*Southern Union Co. v. United States*, 567 U.S. 343, 346, 350–60 (2012)]

Verification date: 2026-08-25 → 2026-10-30 (date of the actual review)

- **2026-10-30: Dual independent source review, with second and third independent checks; owner approved 2026-10-30.** Added the Court's petty-offense limit, under which no Apprendi issue arises for an insubstantial fine.
  - AUTH-SCOTUS-SOUTHERN-UNION-567-US-343, 567 U.S. at 346: "The question here is whether the same rule applies to sentences of criminal fines. We hold that it does."
  - AUTH-SCOTUS-SOUTHERN-UNION-567-US-343, 567 U.S. at 350: "Where a fine is so insubstantial that the underlying offense is considered “petty,” the Sixth Amendment right of jury trial is not triggered, and no Apprendi issue arises."
  - AUTH-SCOTUS-SOUTHERN-UNION-567-US-343, 567 U.S. at 360: "We hold that the rule of Apprendi applies to the imposition of criminal fines."

#### CLM-SENT-046: rewritten

Before (public 1.2.1):

> As soon as practical after guilt is established, the court must determine the required property nexus or money judgment under the governing forfeiture statute, using evidence already in the record and any additional relevant and reliable evidence the parties submit. [Fed. R. Crim. P. 32.2(b)(1)]
