#### CLM-CIRDC-005: rewritten

Before (public 1.2.1):

> In the D.C. Circuit, audita querela cannot rest on pure equity and, if it survives at all, can reach only a legal objection not cognizable through existing federal postconviction remedies; a claim cognizable under § 2255 belongs there. [*United States v. Ayala*, 894 F.2d 425, 427–30 (D.C. Cir. 1990)]

After:

> In the D.C. Circuit, a federal court can vacate a conviction through audita querela "only if the writ permits a defendant to raise a legal objection not cognizable under existing federal postconviction remedies," and *Ayala* did not decide whether the writ ever supplies that basis; it called the authority to use a "pure equity" variant "open to serious doubt" without resolving the point, because the appellant had abandoned his equitable claims. A claim cognizable under § 2255 belongs there. [*United States v. Ayala*, 894 F.2d 425, 429–30 (D.C. Cir. 1990)]
