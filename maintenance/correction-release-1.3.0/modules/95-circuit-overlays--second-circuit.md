#### CLM-CIR2-006: rewritten

Before (public 1.2.1):

> In the Second Circuit, audita querela supplies at most a hypothetical avenue when the complete absence of collateral review would raise serious constitutional questions; a nonretroactive Booker claim did not create that circumstance or a remedial gap. [*United States v. Richter*, 510 F.3d 103, 104 (2d Cir. 2007)]

After:

> In the Second Circuit, audita querela "remains available in limited circumstances with respect to criminal convictions": it "is probably available" for a legal objection that arose after the conviction and is not redressable through another post-conviction remedy, and it would lie if the absence of any avenue of collateral attack would raise serious constitutional questions. Because *Booker* is not retroactive on collateral review, Richter presented no colorable constitutional claim, so the absence of other avenues raised no serious constitutional questions and the writ did not lie. [*United States v. Richter*, 510 F.3d 103, 104 (2d Cir. 2007)]
