#### CLM-2241F-005: rewritten

Before (public 1.2.1):

> Section 2241(e) addresses specified alien enemy-combatant detention and transfer decisions. Its specialized text should not be treated as a general limitation on an ordinary federal sentence-execution petition, and constitutional applications outside this module require their own authority. [28 U.S.C. § 2241(e)]

After:

> Section 2241(e) withdraws court jurisdiction over a habeas application filed by or on behalf of an alien detained by the United States who has been determined by the United States to have been properly detained as an enemy combatant or is awaiting that determination, and, except as provided in section 1005(e)(2)–(3) of the Detainee Treatment Act of 2005, over any other action against the United States or its agents relating to any aspect of the detention, transfer, treatment, trial, or conditions of confinement of such an alien who is or was detained; the Code's constitutionality note refers readers to the Table of Laws Held Unconstitutional in Whole or in Part by the Supreme Court, on the Constitution Annotated website, for certain provisions of this section as added and amended by Pub. L. 109-148 and Pub. L. 109-366. Its specialized text should not be treated as a general limitation on an ordinary federal sentence-execution petition, and constitutional applications outside this module require their own authority. [28 U.S.C. § 2241(e) & Constitutionality note]
