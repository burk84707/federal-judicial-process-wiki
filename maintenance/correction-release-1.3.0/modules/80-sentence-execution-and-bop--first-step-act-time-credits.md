#### CLM-FTC-005: rewritten

Before (public 1.2.1):

> A prisoner at minimum or low recidivism risk earns an additional five days for every thirty days of successful participation when the statutory two-assessment condition is met; the regulation expresses that condition as maintaining a consistent minimum or low risk over the two most recent consecutive assessments.

After:

> A prisoner earns an additional five days of time credits for every thirty days of successful participation if the Bureau determines the prisoner to be at minimum or low risk for recidivating and, in the statute's words, the prisoner "over 2 consecutive assessments, has not increased their risk of recidivism"; the regulation words this condition differently, requiring that the inmate have "maintained a consistent minimum or low risk of recidivism over the most recent two consecutive risk and needs assessments." [18 U.S.C. § 3632(d)(4)(A)(ii)]; [28 C.F.R. § 523.42(c)(2)]

Verification date: 2026-08-24 → 2026-10-30 (date of the actual review)

- **2026-10-30: Dual independent source review; owner approved 2026-10-30.** Quoted the statutory two-assessment test and the regulation's differently worded test side by side, instead of saying the regulation expresses the statutory condition, because the reviewers found the two tests are not identical.
  - AUTH-USC-18-3632, 18 U.S.C. § 3632(d)(4)(A)(ii): "A prisoner determined by the Bureau of Prisons to be at a minimum or low risk for recidivating, who, over 2 consecutive assessments, has not increased their risk of recidivism, shall earn an additional 5 days of time credits"
  - AUTH-CFR-28-523-SUBPART-E, 28 C.F.R. § 523.42(c)(2)(i)–(ii): "Has maintained a consistent minimum or low risk of recidivism over the most recent two consecutive risk and needs assessments conducted by the Bureau."

#### CLM-FTC-008: rewritten

Before (public 1.2.1):

> Since September 30, 2026, 28 C.F.R. § 523.42(a) provides that an eligible inmate begins earning FSA Time Credits after the term of imprisonment commences. The amendment removed the earlier clause that tied commencement to arrival or voluntary surrender at the designated Bureau facility, which courts had held conflicts with the First Step Act. A sentence commences under 18 U.S.C. § 3585(a) when the defendant is received in custody awaiting transportation to, or arrives voluntarily at, the official detention facility. Earning still requires successful participation in assigned programs or productive activities. [91 FR 55740 (Aug. 31, 2026)]; [Miles v. Bowers, No. 25-1291 (1st Cir. Apr. 27, 2026)]; [18 U.S.C. § 3585(a)]

After:

> Under the regulation as amended effective September 30, 2026, an eligible inmate begins earning FSA Time Credits after the inmate's term of imprisonment commences; the amendment removed the former parenthetical that identified that date as the date the inmate arrives or voluntarily surrenders at the designated Bureau facility where the sentence will be served. [28 C.F.R. § 523.42(a)]; [91 Fed. Reg. 55,740, 55,741, 55,743 (Aug. 31, 2026) (interim final rule)]

Authorities: AUTH-CFR-28-523-SUBPART-E, AUTH-FR-91-55740, AUTH-CA1-MILES-BOWERS-2026, AUTH-USC-18-3585 → AUTH-CFR-28-523-SUBPART-E-CURRENT, AUTH-FR-91-55740

Verification date: 2026-10-15 → 2026-10-30 (date of the actual review)

- **2026-10-30: Legal review recommendations, each checked against its source text; owner approved 2026-10-30.** Found during verification of the legal review: the claim stated the designated-facility trigger that the Sept. 30, 2026 interim final rule removed from § 523.42(a).
  - AUTH-FR-91-55740, 91 Fed. Reg. at 55,743 (§ 523.42(a)): "An eligible inmate begins earning FSA Time Credits after the inmate's term of imprisonment commences."

#### CLM-FTC-011: rewritten

Before (public 1.2.1):

> Section 3632(d)(4)(C) directs that earned FTCs be applied toward prerelease custody or supervised release and requires transfer of prisoners who are eligible under § 3624(g); earning a credit and qualifying to apply it are therefore separate determinations.

After:

> Section 3632(d)(4)(C) directs that earned FTCs be applied toward prerelease custody or supervised release and requires the Director to transfer eligible prisoners, as determined under § 3624(g); earning a credit and qualifying to apply it are therefore separate determinations. #### CLM-FTC-014: rewritten

Before (public 1.2.1):

> For early transfer to supervised release, the prisoner must satisfy the general application criteria, have minimum or low risk at the last assessment, and have a court-imposed supervised-release term; FTCs cannot advance that transfer by more than twelve months.

After:

> Under the regulation, the Bureau may apply FTCs toward early transfer to supervised release only when the inmate meets the general application criteria in § 523.44(b) and the prerelease-custody criteria in § 523.44(c) (minimum or low risk through the last two assessments, or an approved warden petition), has maintained minimum or low risk through the last assessment, and has a court-imposed supervised-release term; the transfer may be no earlier than 12 months before it would otherwise have occurred. The statute likewise provides that the Director "may transfer" the prisoner to supervised release early, "not to exceed 12 months." [18 U.S.C. § 3624(g)(1)(D)(ii), (g)(3)]; [28 C.F.R. § 523.44(b)–(d)]

Verification date: 2026-08-24 → 2026-10-30 (date of the actual review)

- **2026-10-30: Dual independent source review; owner approved 2026-10-30.** Added § 523.44(d)'s requirement that the paragraph (c) criteria also be met and the statute's discretionary "may transfer" wording, both identified by the reviewers as omitted, and added citation links.
  - AUTH-CFR-28-523-SUBPART-E, 28 C.F.R. § 523.44(d): "only when an eligible inmate has, in addition to satisfying the criteria in paragraphs (b) and (c) of this section"
  - AUTH-CFR-28-523-SUBPART-E, 28 C.F.R. § 523.44(c)(1)–(2): "Maintained a minimum or low recidivism risk through his or her last two risk and needs assessments; or (2) Had a petition to be transferred to prerelease custody or supervised release approved by the Warden"
  - AUTH-CFR-28-523-SUBPART-E, 28 C.F.R. § 523.44(d)(1)–(3): "has a term of supervised release after imprisonment included as part of his or her sentence as imposed by the sentencing court"
  - AUTH-CFR-28-523-SUBPART-E, 28 C.F.R. § 523.44(d)(3): "would result in transfer to supervised release no earlier than 12 months before the date that transfer to supervised release would otherwise have occurred."
  - AUTH-USC-18-3624, 18 U.S.C. § 3624(g)(1)(D)(ii): "the prisoner has been determined under the System to be a minimum or low risk to recidivate pursuant to the last reassessment of the prisoner."
  - AUTH-USC-18-3624, 18 U.S.C. § 3624(g)(3): "may transfer the prisoner to begin any such term of supervised release at an earlier date, not to exceed 12 months"
