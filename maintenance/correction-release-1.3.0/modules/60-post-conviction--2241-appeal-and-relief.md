#### CLM-2241-001: rewritten

Before (public 1.2.1):

> Section 2253(a) makes a final order in a district-court habeas proceeding subject to review in the court of appeals for that circuit.

After:

> Section 2253(a) makes a final order in a district-court habeas proceeding subject to review in the court of appeals for the circuit in which the proceeding is held. Section 2253(b) allows no appeal from a final order in a proceeding to test the validity of a warrant to remove a person charged with a criminal offense against the United States to another district or place for commitment or trial, or of that person's detention pending removal proceedings, and § 2253(c)(1) requires a certificate of appealability for an appeal from the final order in a habeas proceeding arising out of state-court process or in a § 2255 proceeding. [28 U.S.C. § 2253(a)–(c)]

Verification date: 2026-08-25 → 2026-10-30 (date of the actual review)

  - AUTH-USC-28-2253, § 2253(b): "There shall be no right of appeal from a final order in a proceeding to test the validity of a warrant to remove to another district or place for commitment or trial"

#### CLM-2241-004: rewritten

Before (public 1.2.1):

> The same classification applies when a federal prisoner invokes § 2255(e)'s saving clause: the character of the underlying challenge does not convert the § 2241 proceeding into a § 2255 proceeding for § 2253(c).

After:

> The same classification applies when a federal prisoner invokes § 2255(e)'s saving clause: § 2253(c)(1) imposes the certificate-of-appealability requirement only on state-process habeas and § 2255 final orders, and in *Bruce*, a saving-clause appeal, the Third Circuit stated that the § 2253(c) requirements do not apply to prisoners appealing the denial of a § 2241 petition. *Jones v. Hendrix* later held that the saving clause does not authorize the *Dorsainvil* statutory-claim pathway *Bruce* applied, so *Bruce* is cited here only for its certificate-of-appealability statement. [28 U.S.C. § 2253(c)(1)]; [*Bruce*, 868 F.3d 170]; [*Jones*, 599 U.S. at 477]
