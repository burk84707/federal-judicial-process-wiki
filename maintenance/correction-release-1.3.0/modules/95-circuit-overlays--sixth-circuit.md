> In the Sixth Circuit, § 3583(e)(1) does not make exceptionally good behavior an absolute prerequisite to early termination, though such behavior remains a relevant consideration, and the court said it “might expect” district courts to “generally” find early termination proper only when exceptionally good conduct or other changed circumstances are present. [*United States v. Hale*, No. 24-5362, slip op. at 3–5 (6th Cir.
