#### CLM-CIR8-005: rewritten

Before (public 1.2.1):

> In the Eighth Circuit, Rule 32.1(c)(1) does not require counsel or a hearing before a court denies a motion to terminate supervision or modify conditions, and § 3583(e) does not require a separate explanation for denial when the existing record makes the court's basis discernible. [*United States v. Norris*, 62 F.4th 441, 445–51 (8th Cir. 2023)]

After:

> In the Eighth Circuit, Rule 32.1(c)(1) applies only when a court modifies conditions, so the rule does not require a hearing or counsel before a court denies a motion to terminate supervision or modify conditions. Applying Mosby, Norris held that a district court that had presided over the case since its inception was not required to explain its denial of early termination of supervised release, and acknowledged that the Eighth Circuit “is routinely cited as the outlier” on this point. [*United States v. Norris*, 62 F.4th 441, slip op. at 8, 13–15 & n.4 (8th Cir. 2023)]
