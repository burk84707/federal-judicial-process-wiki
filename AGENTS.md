Read README.md, PUBLICATION_POLICY.md, maintenance/README.md and release.json before work.
Canonical legal content is in docs/, authorities/, data/ and sources/. Generated dist/ is never canonical.
Preserve exact claims, citations, jurisdiction, qualifications, stable IDs and verification dates. A successful build or HTTP request is not legal verification. Never promote unresolved items automatically.
Do not import private archives, original migration filenames, local machine paths, execution logs, credentials, or other organizations' product material. Original research remains separately preserved.
Build and test before handoff. Keep generated artifacts and review reports out of Git. No commit, push, deployment, baseline acceptance or reuse-license grant until owner approval.
