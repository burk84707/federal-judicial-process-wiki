# Sixth Amendment

## Holding or rule used

- In all criminal prosecutions the accused has the right to a speedy and public trial by an impartial jury of the State and district where the crime was committed (district previously ascertained by law), to be informed of the accusation, to confrontation, to compulsory process, and to the assistance of counsel.
