# Trial venue clause

## Holding or rule used

- Trials of all crimes, except impeachment, are by jury and held in the State where the crime was committed; crimes committed outside any State are tried where Congress by law directs.

Constitutional text only; the Constitution Annotated commentary on the page is not cited for any proposition.
