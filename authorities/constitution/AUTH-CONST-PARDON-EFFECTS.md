# Legal Effect of a Pardon

## Holding or rule used

Official historical and doctrinal analysis showing that pardon effects are proposition- and context-specific rather than a universal record-erasure rule.

## Material limitation

Use only for the bounded proposition in the linked modules. The exact judgment, jurisdiction, circuit, custody, other remedies, current agency instructions, requested relief, grant terms, and independent law remain separate where identified.

## Official source and pinpoints

Constitution Annotated, Art. II, § 2, cl. 1, Legal Effect of a Pardon: <https://constitution.congress.gov/browse/essay/artII-S2-C1-3-7/ALDE_00013324/>
