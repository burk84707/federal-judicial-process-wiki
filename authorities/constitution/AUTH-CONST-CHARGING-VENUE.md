# Constitutional Charging and Venue Text

## Propositions supported

The Fifth Amendment supplies the federal grand-jury charging clause and its military exception. Article III and the Sixth Amendment supply the state-of-offense venue and district-vicinage text for federal criminal trials.

## Material limitations

Application requires Supreme Court precedent, the charged offense's conduct elements, statutes enacted by Congress, the selected district, and the procedural record.
