# Attorney General Blanche Launches New Process to Restore Federal Firearm Rights

## Holding or rule used

- Relief under the 18 U.S.C. § 925(c) process removes only applicable federal firearms disabilities and does not override independent restrictions imposed by state law; restoration is neither automatic nor guaranteed.

## Material limitation

Press release, not binding law; cite the § 925(c) rule itself for eligibility standards.
