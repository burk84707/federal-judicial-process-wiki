# Federal Firearm Rights Restoration Frequently Asked Questions

## Holding or rule used

- A full presidential pardon removes penalties and disabilities, including a firearm disability; a person convicted of a federal offense who also has a separate, independent basis for firearm prohibition may need to seek federal firearms relief.

## Material limitation

Nonbinding agency guidance; the statute, 18 U.S.C. § 925(c), and any implementing regulation control.
