# United States v. D'Amario

## Holdings used

- A pending appeal from a supervised-release revocation does not divest the district court of continuing § 3583(e)(2) jurisdiction to modify release conditions during the supervision term.

## Material limitation

Binding only in the First Circuit and only for the bounded procedural postures in the linked claims. Official published opinion and public full-text mirror reviewed. Only continuing condition-modification jurisdiction during the pending revocation appeal is adopted; no early-termination or changed-circumstances rule is inferred. A comprehensive commercial citator was not run.
