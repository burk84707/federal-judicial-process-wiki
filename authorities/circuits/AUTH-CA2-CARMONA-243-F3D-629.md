# Carmona v. U.S. Bureau of Prisons

## Holdings used

- Federal prisoners ordinarily must exhaust before § 2241 review; a closed administrative path caused by procedural default bars review absent cause and prejudice.

## Material limitation

Binding only in the Second Circuit and only for the bounded procedural postures in the linked claims. The original 2001 opinion was not located in the current official Second Circuit archive.
