# United States v. Naranjo

## Holdings used

- Section 404(c)'s successive-motion bar is a mandatory claim-processing rule, not jurisdictional, and must be enforced when properly invoked after a complete prior merits review.

## Material limitation

Binding only in the Fifth Circuit and only for the bounded procedural postures in the linked claims. Official published opinion reviewed. The holding is limited to § 404(c)'s successive-motion bar and does not define every prior disposition that qualifies as a complete merits review. A comprehensive commercial citator was not run.
