# United States v. Carter

## Holdings used

- After Concepcion, the court must consider nonfrivolous intervening legal or factual arguments and give a brief reason; an unexplained denial can require vacatur and remand.

## Material limitation

Binding only in the Ninth Circuit and only for the bounded procedural postures in the linked claims. Official published opinion reviewed.
