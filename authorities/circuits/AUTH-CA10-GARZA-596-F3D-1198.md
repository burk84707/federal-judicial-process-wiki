# Garza v. Davis

## Holdings used

- BOP exhaustion is a prerequisite to § 2241 relief despite no express statutory clause; the futility exception is narrow and higher administrative review defeated the asserted local-bias theory.

## Material limitation

Binding only in the Tenth Circuit and only for the bounded procedural postures in the linked claims. Official published opinion reviewed. The holding is limited to BOP placement review and the futility arguments actually rejected. A comprehensive commercial citator was not run.
