# United States v. Norris

## Holdings used

- Rule 32.1(c)(1) does not require counsel or a hearing before denial of termination or modification, and a separate denial explanation is unnecessary when the record makes the court's basis discernible.

## Material limitation

Binding only in the Eighth Circuit and only for the bounded procedural postures in the linked claims. Official published opinion reviewed. The holdings concern denial: an actual condition modification ordinarily triggers Rule 32.1, and Norris does not approve a record from which no basis can be discerned. A comprehensive commercial citator was not run.
