# Miles v. Bowers

## Holding or rule used

The view in former 28 C.F.R. § 523.42(a) that a sentence commences for time-credit purposes upon arrival at the designated facility conflicts with the text of the First Step Act.

## Material limitation

Cited here only for its holding on former § 523.42(a), which the Bureau then amended.

## Official source and pinpoints

Miles v. Bowers, No. 25-1291, slip op. at 14 (1st Cir. Apr. 27, 2026), CourtListener: <https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/10848657/miles-v-bowers/>
