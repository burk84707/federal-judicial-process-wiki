# Dragenice v. Ridge

## Holdings used

- Administrative exhaustion can make a § 2241 petition premature, but it is a judicial restraint rather than a jurisdictional prerequisite to the district court's habeas power.

## Material limitation

Binding only in the Fourth Circuit and only for the bounded procedural postures in the linked claims. Official published opinion reviewed. The case concerns immigration detention and a pending removal proceeding, not BOP Part 542, so only the bounded nonjurisdictional proposition is used. A comprehensive commercial citator was not run.
