# United States v. Werber

## Holding or rule used

- Rule 36 authorizes a court to correct only clerical errors in the transcription of judgments, not to effectuate unexpressed sentencing intentions; it permits conforming the written judgment to the oral sentence but not amending the oral sentence itself, and it provides no jurisdiction to correct an error of law, including a mistaken failure to depart under the Guidelines.
