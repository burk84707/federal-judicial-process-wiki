# United States v. Caillier

## Holdings used

- Changed circumstances are not a jurisdictional prerequisite to § 3583(e)(2) condition modification, while illegality alone is not an enumerated ground that can itself support modification.

## Material limitation

Binding only in the Fifth Circuit and only for the bounded procedural postures in the linked claims. Official published opinion reviewed. A statutory purpose such as deterrence or public safety may support modification even when an invalid condition prompted review; § 3583(e)(2) does not replace direct or collateral review of legality. A comprehensive commercial citator was not run.
