# United States v. Portillo

## Holding or rule used

- Rule 36 may not be used to make a substantive alteration to a criminal sentence, but a court may correct clerical errors in the written judgment at any time, for example to conform it to the oral sentence; a minor, mechanical correction that does not make the sentence more onerous is clerical (363 F.3d at 1164–65).
