# United States v. Lawrence

## Holdings used

- A defendant has no categorical right to allocution before the district court decides a § 404 sentence-reduction motion.

## Material limitation

Binding only in the D.C. Circuit and only for the bounded procedural postures in the linked claims. Official published opinion reviewed. Only the no-categorical-allocution proposition is used; Smith and Concepcion supply later explanation and review rules. A comprehensive commercial citator was not run.
