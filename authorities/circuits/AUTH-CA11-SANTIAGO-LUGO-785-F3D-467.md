# Santiago-Lugo v. Warden

## Holdings used

- Section 2241 administrative exhaustion is not jurisdictional but remains enforceable when properly asserted; a court need not raise it sua sponte.

## Material limitation

Binding only in the Eleventh Circuit and only for the bounded procedural postures in the linked claims. Official published opinion reviewed; use only for the bounded § 2241 exhaustion proposition. A comprehensive commercial citator was not run.
