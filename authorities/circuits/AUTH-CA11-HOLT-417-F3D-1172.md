# United States v. Holt

## Holdings used

- Audita querela may not be granted when the challenge is cognizable under § 2255; after recharacterization, an unauthorized successive motion remains outside district-court jurisdiction.

## Material limitation

Binding only in the Eleventh Circuit and only for the bounded procedural postures in the linked claims. Official published opinion reviewed. Holt involved a constitutional sentencing attack by a federal prisoner with a prior § 2255 motion; it did not decide survival for claims outside § 2255's field. A comprehensive commercial citator was not run.
