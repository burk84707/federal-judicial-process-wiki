# United States v. Richter

## Holdings used

- A complete lack of collateral review might support audita querela only if it raised serious constitutional questions; Booker nonretroactivity did not create that circumstance.

## Material limitation

Binding only in the Second Circuit and only for the bounded procedural postures in the linked claims. Published per curiam full opinion reviewed from a public court-document reproduction after no stable historical official opinion file was located. Richter does not hold that the writ lies in any actual case and predates Jones v. Hendrix; no post-Jones exception is inferred. A comprehensive commercial citator was not run.
