# United States v. Cordero

## Holdings used

- Denials of § 3583(e) modification and early termination are reviewed for abuse of discretion; the record must permit meaningful review.
- Section 3583(e)(2) is not a vehicle to collaterally attack the original legality of a supervised-release condition.
