# Sawyer v. Holder

## Holdings used

- By negative implication from § 2253(c)(1)(B), a federal prisoner proceeding under § 2241 does not need a COA.

## Material limitation

Binding only in the Eleventh Circuit and only for the bounded procedural postures in the linked claims. Official published opinion reviewed. Only the COA proposition is adopted; later Supreme Court law controls saving-clause merits. A comprehensive commercial citator was not run.
