# United States v. Holder

## Holdings used

- Assuming audita querela survives in federal criminal cases, it requires a legal objection arising after conviction that is not redressable through another postconviction remedy; purely equitable consequences are insufficient.

## Material limitation

Binding only in the First Circuit and only for the bounded procedural postures in the linked claims. Published precedential full opinion reviewed from a public reporter reproduction after no stable historical official opinion file was located. Holder expressly left survival of the writ unresolved, rejected a pure-equity immigration-consequence theory, and identified no actual gap. A 2021 official First Circuit published opinion continued to cite Holder without adverse treatment. A comprehensive commercial citator was not run.
