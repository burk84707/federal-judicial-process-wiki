# United States v. Ayala

## Holdings used

- Audita querela cannot rest on pure equity and, if it survives, can reach only a legal objection not cognizable through existing federal postconviction remedies; claims cognizable under § 2255 belong there.

## Material limitation

Binding only in the D.C. Circuit and only for the bounded procedural postures in the linked claims. Published precedential full opinion reviewed from a public reporter reproduction after no stable historical official opinion file was located. Ayala did not finally decide survival, rejected a pure-equity immigration-consequence theory, and remanded for § 2255 treatment without deciding constitutional merits. A comprehensive commercial citator was not run.
