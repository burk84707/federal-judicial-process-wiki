# Nguyen v. United States

## Propositions supported

- The District Court for the Northern Mariana Islands is an Article IV territorial court; the District Court of Guam was created under Article IV, § 3 (n.3, quoting Guam v. Olsen).

## Material limitations

Use for the courts' constitutional character only; jurisdiction and procedure are governed by statute.
