# Sell v. United States

## Holding used

Rare-case conditions for medication solely to restore trial competency, priority of alternative grounds, and collateral-order appealability.

## Material limitation

The medical evidence, charged offense, likely sentence, confinement, dangerousness, alternatives, side effects, and circuit procedure control.
