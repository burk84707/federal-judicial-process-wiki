# Oregon v. Kennedy

## Holding used

After a defendant-requested mistrial, governmental misconduct bars retrial only when intended to provoke the mistrial request.

## Material limitation

Harassment, overreaching, or prejudicial error without the required objective intent does not alone satisfy this narrow exception.
