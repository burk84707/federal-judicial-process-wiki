# Fernandez v. United States

## Proposition supported

The supposed invalidity of a conviction is not an extraordinary and compelling reason under § 3582(c)(1)(A); § 2255 is the designated federal collateral vehicle.

## Material limitation

The opinion does not prevent a fact from having a distinct permissible compassionate-release relevance that does not ask the court to adjudicate conviction validity.
