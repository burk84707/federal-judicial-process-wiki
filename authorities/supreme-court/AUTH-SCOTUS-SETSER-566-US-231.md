# Setser v. United States

## Holding or rule used

A federal court may order a sentence concurrent with or consecutive to an anticipated state sentence, with BOP administration and review for later implementation disputes.

## Material limitation

Use only for the bounded proposition and limitations in the linked canonical modules. Individual custody, computation, program, placement, administrative, circuit, and remedy facts remain separate.
