# Ohler v. United States

## Holding used

A defendant who introduces a prior conviction on direct examination cannot claim on appeal that its admission was error.

## Material limitation

The rule concerns a party's own introduction of the evidence after the in-limine ruling; different objections and offers require separate preservation analysis.
