# Concepcion v. United States

## Holdings used

- A district court may consider intervening changes of law or fact when exercising discretion under First Step Act § 404.
- It must consider nonfrivolous arguments the parties present, but need not be persuaded and need provide only a brief statement of reasons.
- The benchmark Guidelines range may be recalculated only to reflect retroactive application of the Fair Sentencing Act; other changes may inform the later discretionary choice.

## Material limitations

The Court did not require a sentence reduction. The case decided a § 404 motion, not compassionate release. Its benchmark limitation forecloses treating the proceeding as an unrestricted plenary resentencing.
