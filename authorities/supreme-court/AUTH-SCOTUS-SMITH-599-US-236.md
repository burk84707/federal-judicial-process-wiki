# Smith v. United States

## Holding used

Reversal for a Venue Clause or Vicinage Clause violation permits retrial in the proper venue; the error does not itself require a judgment barring reprosecution.

## Material limitation

The decision addresses remedy after venue error. It does not replace the element-based analysis for deciding where the charged crime was committed.
