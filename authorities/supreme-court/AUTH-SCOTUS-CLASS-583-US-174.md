# Class v. United States

## Holding used

A guilty plea by itself did not bar a direct constitutional attack on the statute of conviction that challenged government power without contradicting the charge, plea, or admitted facts.

## Material limitation

The decision does not preserve all pre-plea claims and does not override an express agreement waiver or preservation requirement.
