# Bloate v. United States

## Holding used

Pretrial-motion preparation time is not automatically excluded under § 3161(h)(1), though a compliant ends-of-justice continuance can exclude it.

## Material limitation

Filing-to-disposition motion time and other periods have separate statutory treatment.
