# United States v. Perez

## Holding used

A court may discharge a genuinely deadlocked jury and retry the defendant when manifest necessity or the ends of public justice support that course.

## Material limitation

Manifest necessity is record- and cause-specific; a hung jury does not authorize a casual or automatic mistrial.
