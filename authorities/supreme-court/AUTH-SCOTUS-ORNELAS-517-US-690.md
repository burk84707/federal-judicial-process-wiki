# Ornelas v. United States

## Holding used

Ultimate reasonable-suspicion and probable-cause determinations receive independent appellate review; historical facts receive clear-error review with due weight to local inferences.

## Material limitation

Other suppression questions, credibility decisions, harmlessness, and remedy can have different review rules.
