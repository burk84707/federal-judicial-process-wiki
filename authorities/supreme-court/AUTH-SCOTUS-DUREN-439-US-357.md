# Duren v. Missouri

## Holding used

A prima facie fair-cross-section claim requires a distinctive group, unfair and unreasonable venire representation, and systematic exclusion in the selection process.

## Material limitation

The right concerns jury pools and does not promise a petit jury that mirrors the community; the actual plan and data are essential.
