# Doggett v. United States

## Holding used

Extremely long postindictment government negligence can establish weighty presumptive prejudice within the Barker balance without specifically demonstrable trial prejudice.

## Material limitation

The result turned on extraordinary delay, negligence, lack of acquiescence, and unrebutted prejudice; it is not an automatic rule for every delay.
