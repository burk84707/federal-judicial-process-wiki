# Blanton v. City of North Las Vegas

## Holding used

An offense carrying no more than six months' imprisonment is presumptively petty unless severe additional statutory penalties clearly show a legislative judgment that it is serious.

## Material limitation

Only authorized statutory penalties are assessed; collateral consequences and case-specific exposure must not be assumed to overcome the presumption.
