# Wade v. United States

## Holding or rule used

In the § 3553(e)/USSG § 5K1.1 posture actually decided, a prosecutor's substantial-assistance filing decision is reviewable for unconstitutional motive or lack of rational relation to a legitimate government end, but assistance allegations alone do not require relief, discovery, or a hearing.
