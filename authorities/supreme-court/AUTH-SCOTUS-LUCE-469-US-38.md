# Luce v. United States

## Holding used

A defendant must testify to preserve review of an in-limine Rule 609(a) impeachment ruling.

## Material limitation

The holding is tied to the contingent Rule 609 impeachment setting; other definitive evidentiary rulings are governed by Rule 103 and applicable precedent.

## Official source and pinpoints
