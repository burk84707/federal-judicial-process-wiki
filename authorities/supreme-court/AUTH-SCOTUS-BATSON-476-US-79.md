# Batson v. Kentucky

## Holding used

Equal protection forbids race-based prosecutorial peremptory strikes and supplies a record-dependent burden framework.

## Material limitation

The complete strike, comparative-juror, explanation, and trial-court findings record and later controlling refinements must be examined.
