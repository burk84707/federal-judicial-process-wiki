# United States v. Leon

## Holding used

Deterrence basis of the exclusionary rule and objective-reliance exception for a later-invalidated warrant, with the opinion's four principal limits.

## Material limitation

Later cases refine deterrence and other warrant or nonwarrant contexts; the actual affidavit and officer conduct control.
