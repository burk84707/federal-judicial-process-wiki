# Betterman v. Montana

## Holding used

The Sixth Amendment Speedy Trial Clause does not govern delay after conviction and before sentencing; the Court left any due-process theory unresolved.

## Material limitation

The opinion does not establish a due-process test for sentencing delay or decide bifurcated functional-element questions.
