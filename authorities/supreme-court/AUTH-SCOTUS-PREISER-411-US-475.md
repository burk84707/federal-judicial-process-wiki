# Preiser v. Rodriguez

## Holding used

A state prisoner's attack on the fact or duration of physical confinement seeking immediate or speedier release lies at habeas's core and cannot be brought under § 1983.

## Material limitations

The case concerns state prisoners and the § 1983 boundary. It does not supply every federal-prisoner § 2241 cognizability or remedy rule. A comprehensive commercial citator was not run.
