# United States v. Morgan

## Holding or rule used

Coram nobis remains available under § 1651 as an extraordinary continuation of the original federal criminal case after sentence completion, subject to no-other-remedy, sound-reason, fundamental-error, presumption, and burden limits.

## Material limitation

Use only for the bounded proposition in the linked modules. The exact judgment, jurisdiction, circuit, custody, other remedies, current agency instructions, requested relief, grant terms, and independent law remain separate where identified.
