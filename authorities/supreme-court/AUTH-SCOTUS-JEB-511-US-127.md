# J.E.B. v. Alabama ex rel. T.B.

## Holding used

Intentional sex discrimination through peremptory challenges violates equal protection.

## Material limitation

The decision does not eliminate legitimate sex-neutral strikes; the actual selection record and controlling procedure govern.
