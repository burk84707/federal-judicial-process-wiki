# United States v. Timmreck

## Holding used

A formal Rule 11 violation that is neither constitutional nor jurisdictional does not alone satisfy the § 2255 collateral standard.

## Material limitation

The decision does not bar independently proven constitutional, jurisdictional, or fundamental-defect claims.
