# Peña-Rodriguez v. Colorado

## Holding used

The Sixth Amendment requires an exception to the no-impeachment rule for a clear juror statement showing that racial animus was a significant motivating factor in the vote to convict.

## Material limitation

The threshold is demanding and limited to overt racial bias casting serious doubt on verdict fairness; the Court left procedural implementation primarily to trial courts.
