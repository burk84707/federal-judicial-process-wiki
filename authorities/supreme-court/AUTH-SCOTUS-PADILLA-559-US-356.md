# Padilla v. Kentucky

## Holding used

Counsel must provide correct immigration advice when removal law is clear and at least advise of risk when it is unclear.

## Material limitation

Actual citizenship, statute, conviction, immigration law, advice, reliance, and prejudice require individualized analysis.
