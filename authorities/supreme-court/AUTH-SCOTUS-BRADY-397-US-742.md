# Brady v. United States

## Holding used

A guilty plea must be voluntary and intelligent with sufficient awareness of relevant circumstances and likely direct consequences; lawful plea incentives do not alone make it coerced.

## Material limitation

Voluntariness depends on the actual record, information, advice, promises, threats, competence, and consequences.
