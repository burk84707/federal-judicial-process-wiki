# United States v. Lovasco

## Holding used

Actual prejudice is generally necessary but not sufficient for a due-process preaccusation-delay claim; the reasons for delay also matter. Good-faith investigative delay does not violate due process merely because the defense was somewhat prejudiced, and prosecutors need not charge immediately upon probable cause.

## Material limitation

The Court left lower courts to apply settled due-process principles to particular reasons and records; circuit formulations and the actual evidence must be checked.
