# Tollett v. Henderson

## Holding used

A counseled voluntary and intelligent plea ordinarily forecloses independent collateral inquiry into antecedent constitutional deprivations and focuses review on the plea and counsel's advice.

## Material limitation

Power-to-convict claims, express reservations, agreement waivers, and plea-validity claims require distinct analysis.
