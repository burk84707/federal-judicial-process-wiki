# Dillon v. United States

## Proposition supported

Section 3582(c)(2) uses a two-step inquiry governed at step one by § 1B1.10 and is not a plenary resentencing.

## Material limitation

The case concerns § 3582(c)(2), not every sentence-modification authority.
