# Napue v. Illinois

## Holding used

Due process forbids a prosecutor from knowingly using false testimony or leaving known false testimony uncorrected, including false testimony bearing on a witness's credibility.

## Material limitation

The case involved testimony the prosecutor knew was false and applies its reasonable-likelihood formulation. It should not be collapsed into every ordinary impeachment nondisclosure.
