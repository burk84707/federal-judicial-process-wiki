# Mabry v. Johnson

## Holding used

An unexecuted plea offer has no freestanding constitutional significance, and the defendant had no constitutional right to performance of an offer withdrawn before plea entry.

## Material limitation

The case does not displace counsel duties under later decisions or enforcement of promises that actually induced an entered plea.
