# Pate v. Robinson

## Holding used

Constitutionally sufficient doubt about competency triggers a hearing duty that cannot be defeated by treating the incompetent defendant as having waived it.

## Material limitation

The federal statutory reasonable-cause trigger and circuit descriptions of the record threshold must also be checked.
