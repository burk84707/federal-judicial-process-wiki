# United States v. Ruiz

## Holding used

The Constitution does not require material impeachment disclosure before a plea agreement and did not require pre-bargaining affirmative-defense information on the agreement presented.

## Material limitation

The opinion distinguished information establishing factual innocence and does not erase statutory, rule-based, order-based, or ethical disclosure obligations.
