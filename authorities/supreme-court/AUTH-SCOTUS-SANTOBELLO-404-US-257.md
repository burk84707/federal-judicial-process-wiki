# Santobello v. New York

## Holding used

A prosecution promise that significantly induced a plea must be fulfilled; the state court selected withdrawal or specific-performance resentencing as the case-appropriate remedy.

## Material limitation

The promise, breach, preservation, materiality, and remedy require the agreement and record; the defendant does not automatically choose the remedy.
