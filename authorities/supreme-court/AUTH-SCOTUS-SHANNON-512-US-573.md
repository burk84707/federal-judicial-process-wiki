# Shannon v. United States

## Holding used

No general federal jury instruction on insanity-acquittee commitment consequences, with a bounded corrective-instruction situation for a false impression of immediate release.

## Material limitation

The actual arguments, instruction request, and misstatement determine whether a correction is implicated.
