# Lafler v. Cooper

## Holding used

Effective assistance applies to advice about accepting an offer; proven rejection-stage prejudice and remedy have specific, bounded requirements.

## Material limitation

Deficiency, probable acceptance, prosecutorial and judicial action, relative outcome, and remedy require the actual case record.
