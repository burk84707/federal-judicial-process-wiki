# United States v. Taylor

## Holding used

No presumption favors with- or without-prejudice dismissal under the Speedy Trial Act; the district court must reason through the statutory factors and relevant prejudice.

## Material limitation

The seriousness, circumstances of delay, administration-of-justice effect, prejudice, and record explanation are case-specific.
