# Jackson v. Virginia

## Holding used

Constitutional sufficiency asks whether, viewing the evidence most favorably to the prosecution, any rational factfinder could find every essential element beyond a reasonable doubt.

## Material limitation

The charged elements and complete admitted-evidence record control; the standard does not authorize credibility reweighing.
