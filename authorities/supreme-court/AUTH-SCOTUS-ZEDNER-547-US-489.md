# Zedner v. United States

## Holding used

No blanket prospective Speedy Trial Act waiver; ends-of-justice exclusions require express on-record findings and missing findings are not cured through harmless-error review.

## Material limitation

Each continuance, finding, exclusion, defense request, and dismissal motion must be located on the docket.
