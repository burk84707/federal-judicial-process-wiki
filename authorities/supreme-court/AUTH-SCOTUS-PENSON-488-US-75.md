# Penson v. Ohio

## Holding or rule used

Independent record review and appointment of counsel when an arguable issue exists.

## Material limitation

Use only for the bounded proposition in the linked direct-review module; actual deadlines, records, issues, waivers, local rules, and circuit applications remain separate where identified.
