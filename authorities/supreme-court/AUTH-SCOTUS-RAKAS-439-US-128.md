# Rakas v. Illinois

## Holding used

Fourth Amendment rights are personal, and a privacy-based challenge asks whether the defendant's own legitimate expectation of privacy was invaded.

## Material limitation

The actual property, place, possession, permission, control, and claimed governmental conduct remain fact-specific.
