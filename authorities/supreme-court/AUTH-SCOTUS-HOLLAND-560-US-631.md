# Holland v. Florida

## Holding or rule used

Equitable tolling of § 2244(d), with diligence and extraordinary-circumstance requirements.

## Material limitation

Use only for the bounded proposition in the linked § 2255 module. State-habeas holdings are not silently converted into direct § 2255 holdings; record, circuit, local, deadline, custody, waiver, and classification issues remain separate where identified.
