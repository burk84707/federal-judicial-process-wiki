# Franks v. Delaware

## Holding used

Threshold, proof, materiality, hearing, and suppression rules for specified deliberate or reckless warrant-affidavit falsehoods.

## Material limitation

Omissions, informant statements, extensions beyond the original holding, and record applications require controlling law.
