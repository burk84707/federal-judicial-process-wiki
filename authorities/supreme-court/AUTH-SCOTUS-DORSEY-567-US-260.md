# Dorsey v. United States

## Holding used

The Fair Sentencing Act's lower mandatory minimums apply to pre-Act offenders sentenced on or after August 3, 2010.

## Material limitation

*Dorsey* decided the pipeline-sentencing question. It did not create First Step Act § 404, and § 404 eligibility must be applied from Congress's later text.

## Official source and pinpoint
