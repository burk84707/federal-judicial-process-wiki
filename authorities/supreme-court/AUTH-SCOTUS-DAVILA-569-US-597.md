# United States v. Davila

## Holding used

Judicial participation violates Rule 11(c)(1), but the rule does not require automatic vacatur; Rule 11(h) and the full record control prejudice.

## Material limitation

Preservation, timing, content, context, later proceedings, and the plea decision require case-specific assessment.
