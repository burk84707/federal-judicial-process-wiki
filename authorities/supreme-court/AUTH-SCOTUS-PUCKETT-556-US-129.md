# Puckett v. United States

## Holding used

An unpreserved government-breach claim receives ordinary plain-error review, and later breach does not retroactively invalidate a plea that was knowing and voluntary when entered.

## Material limitation

Agreement construction, breach, prejudice, and remedy remain record- and claim-specific.
