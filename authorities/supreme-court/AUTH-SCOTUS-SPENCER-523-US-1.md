# Spencer v. Kemna

## Holding or rule used

No presumed collateral consequences saved an expired parole-revocation challenge; concrete continuing injury and redressability were required.

## Material limitation

Use only for the bounded proposition in the linked modules. The actual custody, filing posture, respondent, district, circuit law, administrative record, continuing injury, merits, and relief remain separate where identified.
