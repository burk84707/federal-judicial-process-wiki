# United States v. Dominguez Benitez

## Holding used

For an unpreserved Rule 11 error, the defendant must show a reasonable probability that absent the error the defendant would not have pleaded guilty.

## Material limitation

The whole record and the particular omitted advice control; preserved claims use a different allocation.
