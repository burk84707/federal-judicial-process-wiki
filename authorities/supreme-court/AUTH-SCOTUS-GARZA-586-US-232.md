# Garza v. Idaho

## Holding used

No appeal waiver bars every possible claim, and counsel must file a requested notice of appeal; presumed prejudice applies despite the waiver.

## Material limitation

The opinion did not define every permissible waiver exception or decide the merits of any later appeal.
