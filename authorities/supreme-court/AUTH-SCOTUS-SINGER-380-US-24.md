# Singer v. United States

## Holding used

Conditioning federal jury waiver on government consent and court approval does not violate a constitutional right to insist on a bench trial.

## Material limitation

The Court left open a possible exceptional prosecutorial refusal; current Rule 23 and the actual record control.
