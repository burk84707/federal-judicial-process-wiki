# Miranda v. Arizona

## Holding used

Warnings and waiver safeguards for prosecution use of statements stemming from custodial interrogation.

## Material limitation

Custody, interrogation, invocation, waiver, public safety, impeachment, fruits, and two-step questioning have extensive later doctrine.

## Official source and pinpoints

384 U.S. 436, 444–45, 467–79 (1966): <https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-384/pdf/USREPORTS-384-436.pdf>
