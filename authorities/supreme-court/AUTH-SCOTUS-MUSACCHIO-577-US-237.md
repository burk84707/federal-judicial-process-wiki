# Musacchio v. United States

## Holding used

Sufficiency is assessed against the charged offense's elements rather than an erroneously heightened instruction, without reweighing evidence or credibility.

## Material limitation

Instructional error is a separate claim; this holding does not decide its preservation or remedy.
