# Reno v. Koray

## Holding or rule used

Restrictive Bail Reform Act release was not official detention under § 3585(b).

## Material limitation

Use only for the bounded proposition and limitations in the linked canonical modules. Individual custody, computation, program, placement, administrative, circuit, and remedy facts remain separate.

## Official source and pinpoints
