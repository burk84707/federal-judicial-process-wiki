# Irizarry v. United States

## Holding or rule used

Rule 32(h) departure notice does not categorically apply to § 3553(a) variances.

## Material limitation

Use only for the bounded proposition in the linked canonical module; offense-specific, record-specific, circuit-specific, later-proceeding, and negative-treatment questions remain separate where identified.
