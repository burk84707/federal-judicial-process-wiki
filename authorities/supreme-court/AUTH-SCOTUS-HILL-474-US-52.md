# Hill v. Lockhart

## Holding used

Strickland applies to accepted-plea ineffective-assistance claims; prejudice ordinarily requires a reasonable probability that the defendant would have rejected the plea and insisted on trial.

## Material limitation

The alleged error and its objective and defendant-specific effect must be proven on the actual record.
