# Lee v. United States

## Holding used

Accepted-plea prejudice compares consequences and the defendant's rational decision using contemporaneous evidence; a weak trial defense is not an automatic bar.

## Material limitation

The decision was expressly grounded in unusual facts and does not validate post hoc assertion alone.
