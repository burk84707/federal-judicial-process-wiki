# Barker v. Wingo

## Holding used

Sixth Amendment speedy-trial analysis balances length, reason, assertion, and prejudice after sufficient threshold delay; no single factor is necessary or sufficient.

## Material limitation

The accusation date, all delay periods and causes, assertions, incarceration, anxiety, and defense impairment require the full record.
