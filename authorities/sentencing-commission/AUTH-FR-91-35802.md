# Proposed Priorities for Amendment Cycle (notice; retroactivity of 2026 amendments)

## Holding or rule used

- The Commission is not soliciting comment or holding a hearing on retroactivity for any of the amendments submitted to Congress on April 30, 2026; votes for making the inflationary-adjustment amendment retroactive did not meet the four-vote threshold (91 Fed. Reg. at 35,802–03).

## Material limitation

Agency notice; § 1B1.10(d) controls which amendments are retroactive.
