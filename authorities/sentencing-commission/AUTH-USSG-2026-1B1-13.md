# Reduction in Term of Imprisonment Under 18 U.S.C. § 3582(c)(1)(A)

## Holding or rule used

- Compassionate-release categories, no-danger rule, rehabilitation, and legal-change text subject to Rutherford.

## Material limitation

Applies at sentencings on or after Nov. 1, 2026, unless § 1B1.11 and the Ex Post Facto Clause require an earlier edition. Text compared with the 2025 edition; changes in this chapter are limited to Amendments 838, 840, 841, and 843 (91 Fed. Reg. 24,088).
