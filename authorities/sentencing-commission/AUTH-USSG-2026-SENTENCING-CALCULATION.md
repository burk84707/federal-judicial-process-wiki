# 2025 Federal Sentencing Guidelines Calculation Provisions

## Holding or rule used

- Sequenced Guidelines calculation, departures, variances, one-book rule, and ex post facto instruction.

## Material limitation

Applies at sentencings on or after Nov. 1, 2026, unless § 1B1.11 and the Ex Post Facto Clause require an earlier edition. Text compared with the 2025 edition; changes in this chapter are limited to Amendments 838, 840, 841, and 843 (91 Fed. Reg. 24,088).

U.S.S.G. §§ 1B1.1, 1B1.11 (Nov. 1, 2026): <https://www.ussc.gov/guidelines/2026-guidelines-manual/annotated-2026-chapter-1>
