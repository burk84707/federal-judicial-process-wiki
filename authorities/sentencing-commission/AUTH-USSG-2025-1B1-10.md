# U.S.S.G. § 1B1.10 (2025)

## Propositions supported

Listed-amendment retroactivity, actual-range lowering, substitute-only recalculation, reduction floors, substantial-assistance exception, time-served floor, and discretionary considerations in § 3582(c)(2) proceedings.

## Material limitations

The amendment list and text must be rechecked against the manual current on the decision date. This record does not calculate any person's amended range.
