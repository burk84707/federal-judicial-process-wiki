# First Step Act § 102(b)

## Propositions supported

- Amendment of § 3624(b)(1) from time served to sentence imposed.
- Effectiveness when the risk-and-needs assessment system was released.
- Applicability before, on, or after enactment except offenses before November 1, 1987.
