# CARES Act, section 12003

## Holding or rule used

Temporary covered-emergency authority to lengthen home confinement.

## Material limitation

Use only for the bounded proposition and limitations in the linked canonical modules. Individual custody, computation, program, placement, administrative, circuit, and remedy facts remain separate.

## Official source and pinpoints

Pub. L. No. 116-136, § 12003, 134 Stat. 281, 516 (2020), § 12003(a)(2), (b)(2): <https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-116publ136/pdf/PLAW-116publ136.pdf>
