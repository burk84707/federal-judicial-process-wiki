# Federal Jury Selection and Service Act Provisions

## Propositions supported

Random fair-cross-section policy, prohibited bases of jury-service exclusion, and the defendant's timing, sworn-statement, remedy, and exclusive-statutory-challenge provisions.

## Material limitations

The district jury plan, source data, facts supporting substantial noncompliance, constitutional theories, access procedure, and controlling circuit law require separate review.
