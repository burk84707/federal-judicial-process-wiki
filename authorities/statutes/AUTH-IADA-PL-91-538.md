# Interstate Agreement on Detainers Act

## Holding or rule used

Agreement procedures for covered untried charges, custody requests, clocks, and anti-shuttling.

## Material limitation

Use only for the bounded proposition and limitations in the linked canonical modules. Individual custody, computation, program, placement, administrative, circuit, and remedy facts remain separate.

## Official source and pinpoints

Pub. L. No. 91-538, 84 Stat. 1397 (1970), arts. I–IX: <https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/STATUTE-84/pdf/STATUTE-84-Pg1397.pdf>
