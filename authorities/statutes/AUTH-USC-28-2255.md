# 28 U.S.C. § 2255

## Source status

The section text and currentness statement were verified against the Office of the Law Revision Counsel preliminary edition, which reported laws in effect on July 21, 2026. The full text of subsections (a) through (h) was also retrieved from the official 2023 GovInfo United States Code PDF. Currentness is controlled by the OLRC preliminary edition, not the older GovInfo main edition.

## Official sources

- Current preliminary edition: <https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/28/2255>
- Official GovInfo PDF: <https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title28/pdf/USCODE-2023-title28-partVI-chap153-sec2255.pdf>

## Propositions supported

- Subsection (h) requires panel certification as provided in § 2244 for a second or successive motion.
- Subsections (h)(1) and (h)(2) state two alternative substantive gates.

## Material limitations

The statute does not itself decide whether a later filing is legally “second or successive” or whether either substantive gate is satisfied. Those questions require independently verified controlling cases.
