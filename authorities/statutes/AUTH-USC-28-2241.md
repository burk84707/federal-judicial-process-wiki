# 28 U.S.C. § 2241

## Propositions supported

- Subsection (a) identifies courts and judges empowered to grant the writ.
- Subsection (c) identifies custody predicates, including custody in violation of federal law.
- Subsections (b) and (d) contain express transfer provisions for their stated contexts.

## Material limitations

This card does not resolve saving-clause availability, respondent, venue, exhaustion, subsection (e), or a particular remedy.
