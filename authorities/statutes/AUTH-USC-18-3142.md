# 18 U.S.C. § 3142

## Propositions supported

Section 3142 defines the pretrial order sequence, recognizance and conditional-release rules, detention-hearing gates, detention findings, evidentiary standard for safety, rebuttable presumptions, factors, and release and detention orders.

## Material limitations

The statute does not state a burden of proof for flight-based detention. Circuit-specific burdens, presumption effects, evidentiary practices, reopening, and appellate review require separate authority.
