# 18 U.S.C. § 3632

## Propositions supported

- The 210-day public system-development requirement and dynamic reassessment.
- FTC earning rates and pre-enactment or pre-sentence exclusions.
- The enumerated-offense earning bar and final-removal-order application bar.
- The statutory handoff from earning to application under § 3624(g).

## Material limitations

The exact current text controls individualized offense screening. This record does not resolve aggregated sentences, computation disputes, or habeas remedies.
