# 28 U.S.C. § 2253

## Propositions supported

- Subsection (a) makes a final district-court habeas or § 2255 order subject to appellate review.
- Subsection (c)(1)(A) requires a COA for a final habeas order when the detention complained of arises from state-court process.
- Subsection (c)(1)(B) separately requires a COA for a final § 2255 order.
- The statute contains no COA category for a federal-process § 2241 final order.

## Material limitations

Section 2253 does not supply notice timing, local appellate procedure, or interlocutory appealability.
