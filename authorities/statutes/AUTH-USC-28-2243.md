# 28 U.S.C. § 2243

## Propositions supported

- The statute governs the writ or show-cause order, return, hearing, and decision.
- Its final paragraph directs the court to dispose of the matter as law and justice require.

## Material limitations

Section 2243 does not itself create an entitlement to credits, release, placement, or another execution benefit. The substantive source of law and controlling precedent determine the permissible relief.
