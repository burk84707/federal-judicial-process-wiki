# Government Criminal Appeals

## Propositions supported

Government appeal of suppression, exclusion, and property-return orders; certification, timing, diligence, double-jeopardy, and liberal-construction limits.

## Material limitations

Application requires the actual order, jeopardy status, certification, notice, and controlling appellate law.
