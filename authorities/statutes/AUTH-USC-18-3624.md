# 18 U.S.C. § 3624

## Propositions supported

- Release-date relationship in subsection (a).
- Eligibility, maximum rate, conduct and education conditions, last-year text, and vesting in subsection (b).
- Prerelease-custody and supervised-release application criteria in subsection (g).

## Material limitations

The statute must be read with current 28 C.F.R. § 523.20 for GCT mechanics and §§ 523.40–523.44 for FTC application. Individual calculations, administrative review, and judicial remedies are outside this record.
