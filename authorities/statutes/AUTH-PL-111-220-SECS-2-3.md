# Fair Sentencing Act §§ 2–3

## Propositions supported

- Crack threshold changes in 21 U.S.C. §§ 841(b)(1) and 960(b).
- Elimination of the crack-specific mandatory minimum in 21 U.S.C. § 844(a).
