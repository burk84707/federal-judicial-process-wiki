# 18 U.S.C. § 3237

## Proposition supported

Section 3237 supplies the general multi-district and specified continuing-offense venue rule, subject to contrary congressional enactments and subsection (b)'s special tax-election rule.

## Material limitation

Venue still turns on the charged offense's conduct elements and any offense-specific statute. This card does not decide waiver, proof, review, or remedy.
