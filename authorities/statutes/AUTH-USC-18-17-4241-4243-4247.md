# Federal Competency and Insanity Statutes

## Propositions supported

Federal insanity elements and burden; competency trigger, hearing, proof, restoration, and recovery; insanity examination and special verdict; acquittee hearing, burden, commitment, and release; examination periods, reports, and hearing rights.

## Material limitations

Diagnosis, restoration probability, dangerousness, facility placement, medication, privilege, circuit procedure, and the omitted §§ 4244–4246 require their own facts and authority.
