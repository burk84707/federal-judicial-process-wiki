# Designation of State Institutions for Service of Federal Sentence

## Holding or rule used

BOP policy for designating a state institution for concurrent service of a federal sentence and for reviewing nunc pro tunc designation requests after Setser v. United States, 566 U.S. 231 (2012).

## Material limitation

Agency policy does not bind courts and cannot change the governing statutes. Use only for the bounded propositions in the linked module.

## Official source and pinpoints

BOP PS 5160.06 (Sept. 3, 2026), §§ 1–9: <https://www.bop.gov/policy/progstat/5160_006.pdf>
