# About U.S. District Courts

## Proposition used

The official Judiciary source identifies 94 district-level courts, including the three Article IV territorial courts in Guam, the Northern Mariana Islands, and the Virgin Islands.

## Material limitation

This source verifies institutional structure and count. Statutes control jurisdiction and court organization; current local rules, standing orders, and judge-specific practices require separate official review.
