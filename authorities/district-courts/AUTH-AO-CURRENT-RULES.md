# Current Rules of Practice and Procedure

## Proposition used

The official Judiciary source identifies 28 U.S.C. § 2071 and the applicable national procedural rules as authority for federal local rules and explains the public-posting requirement.

## Material limitation

This record establishes the official publication framework. It does not verify the text, currentness, interpretation, or application of a particular local rule, standing order, form, or judge-specific practice.
