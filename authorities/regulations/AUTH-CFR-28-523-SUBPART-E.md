# 28 C.F.R. Part 523, Subpart E

## Propositions supported

- FTC definitions, earning eligibility, earning dates, and ten- or fifteen-day rates.
- The distinction between earning and application, including final removal orders.
- General, prerelease-custody, and supervised-release application criteria.
- Loss, administrative review, and restoration structure.

## Material limitations

The 2025 official annual CFR was cross-checked against the eCFR current through August 21, 2026. Parts 541 and 542 and individual Bureau records are required for case-specific application.
