# First Step Act Time Credits—Revisions

## Holding or rule used

Amended 28 C.F.R. § 523.42(a) to provide that an eligible inmate begins earning FSA Time Credits after the term of imprisonment commences, removing the clause that tied commencement to arrival or voluntary surrender at the designated facility.

## Material limitation

Interim final rule; check for a later final rule.
