# First Step Act Time Credits—Revisions (interim final rule)

## Holding or rule used

- Revises 28 C.F.R. § 523.42(a) so an eligible inmate begins earning FSA Time Credits after the term of imprisonment commences, removing the former parenthetical tied to arrival or voluntary surrender at the designated Bureau facility (91 Fed. Reg. at 55,740–41, 55,743).
- Revises 28 C.F.R. § 523.44(a)(3) so it does not bar applying FSA Time Credits for a term imposed in a foreign country for which the U.S. Parole Commission has determined an equivalent U.S. Code sentence under 18 U.S.C. § 4106A (91 Fed. Reg. at 55,741, 55,743–44).

## Material limitation

Interim final rule with a comment period that closed Sept. 30, 2026; check for a later final rule before relying on it.
