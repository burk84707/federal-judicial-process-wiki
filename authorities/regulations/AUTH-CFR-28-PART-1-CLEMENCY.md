# Executive Clemency

## Holding or rule used

Current advisory DOJ petition, investigation, disposition, effect-notification, and capital-clemency procedures, expressly creating no rights or presidential constraint.

## Material limitation

Use only for the bounded proposition in the linked modules. The exact judgment, jurisdiction, circuit, custody, other remedies, current agency instructions, requested relief, grant terms, and independent law remain separate where identified.

28 C.F.R. pt. 1, 28 C.F.R. §§ 1.1–1.11: <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/chapter-I/part-1>
