# Federal Evidence Rule 410

## Propositions supported

Default inadmissibility of specified pleas and plea-discussion statements against the defendant and the rule's two stated exceptions.

## Material limitations

Whether a communication was a protected plea discussion and whether a valid waiver applies require the actual facts, agreement, and controlling law.

## Official source and pinpoints

Rule 410: <https://www.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/document/federal-rules-of-evidence.pdf>
