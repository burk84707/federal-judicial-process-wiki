# Federal Criminal Rules 23, 24, 26, 30, 31, and 43

## Propositions supported

Jury waiver and size, bench findings, voir dire and peremptory challenges, alternates, open-court testimony, jury instructions and objections, verdict return and unanimity, and defendant presence.

## Material limitations

Local jury plans, individual records, offense-specific instructions, preservation, prejudice, and circuit applications require separate review.

## Official source and pinpoints

Rules 23, 24, 26, 30, 31, and 43: <https://www.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/document/federal-rules-of-criminal-procedure.pdf>
