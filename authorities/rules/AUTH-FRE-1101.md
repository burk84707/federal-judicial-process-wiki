# Applicability of the Federal Rules of Evidence

## Holding or rule used

Except for privilege, the Evidence Rules do not apply to proceedings granting or revoking supervised release.

## Material limitation

Use only for the bounded proposition and express limitations in the linked canonical modules. Offense, judgment, term, condition, jurisdiction, violation record, circuit law, and requested relief remain separate.

## Official source and pinpoints

Fed. R. Evid. 1101, Rule 1101(c), (d)(3): <https://www.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/2025-02/federal-rules-of-evidence-dec-1-2024.pdf>
