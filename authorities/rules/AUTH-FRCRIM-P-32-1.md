# Revoking or modifying probation or supervised release

## Holding or rule used

Current national procedure for initial appearance, transfer, detention, preliminary and revocation hearings, modification, disposition, and Rule 26.2.

## Material limitation

Use only for the bounded proposition and express limitations in the linked canonical modules. Offense, judgment, term, condition, jurisdiction, violation record, circuit law, and requested relief remain separate.
