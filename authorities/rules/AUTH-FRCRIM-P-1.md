# Federal Rule of Criminal Procedure 1

## Proposition supported

The Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure govern criminal proceedings in United States district courts, courts of appeals, and the Supreme Court.

## Material limitation

Rule 1 supplies the rules' institutional scope. Rule 33 supplies the particular new-trial procedure.
