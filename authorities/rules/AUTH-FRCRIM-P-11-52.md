# Federal Criminal Rules 11 and 52

## Propositions supported

Federal plea types, conditional pleas, colloquy, factual basis, agreement types, acceptance and rejection, withdrawal, recordkeeping, harmless error, and plain error.

## Material limitations

The actual agreement, record, preservation, offense, sentence, and controlling appellate law govern application. A pending proposal is not current law.
