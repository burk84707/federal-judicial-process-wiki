# Federal Evidence Rules 103 and 104

## Propositions supported

Evidentiary objections, offers of proof, definitive rulings, plain-error recognition, and preliminary admissibility questions.

## Material limitations

The particular evidence rule and controlling precedent supply the merits. The official rules are amended through December 1, 2025.
