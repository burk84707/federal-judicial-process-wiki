# Federal Evidence Rule 606

## Propositions supported

Juror competence as a witness and the no-impeachment rule, including its three textual exceptions.

## Material limitations

Admissibility of particular proof, nonjuror evidence, constitutional exceptions, local juror-contact rules, procedure, and prejudice require separate analysis.

## Official source and pinpoints

Rule 606: <https://www.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/document/federal-rules-of-evidence.pdf>
