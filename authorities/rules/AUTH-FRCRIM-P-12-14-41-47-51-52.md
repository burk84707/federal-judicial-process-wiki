# Current Federal Criminal Pretrial-Motion Rules

## Propositions supported

Required pretrial motions, deadlines, rulings, suppression hearings, mental-condition notice, severance, search-warrant motions, motion form, preservation, and error classification.

## Material limitations

Local deadlines, particular constitutional merits, and circuit applications require separate authority. The current-rules page states the Criminal Rules were last amended in 2023.

## Official source and pinpoints

Rules 12, 12.2, 14, 41, 47, 51, and 52: <https://www.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/document/federal-rules-of-criminal-procedure.pdf>
